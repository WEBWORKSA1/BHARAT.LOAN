Official sources

Official government portals for business schemes

Apply only on official websites. Here are the direct links for registration, central schemes and state schemes — checked and kept in one place.

Stay safe: government portals end in .gov.in, .nic.in or are run by public institutions (e.g. SIDBI, NSIC). Never pay an “agent fee” for approval, and never share OTPs or banking passwords.

Registration & basics

udyamregistration.gov.in

Udyam Registration (MSME)

Free MSME registration — required by most schemes.

www.myscheme.gov.in

myScheme

Government’s national scheme discovery platform.

www.jansamarth.in

JanSamarth

Single portal for credit-linked government schemes.

www.udyamimitra.in

Udyami Mitra

SIDBI portal for MSME loan applications and handholding.

champions.gov.in

CHAMPIONS (MSME grievances)

Ministry of MSME grievance and support portal.

Central schemes

www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal

PMEGP e-portal (KVIC)

Apply online for PMEGP margin-money loans.

www.mudra.org.in

MUDRA

Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana information.

www.cgtmse.in

CGTMSE

Credit guarantee for collateral-free MSME loans.

www.standupmitra.in

Stand-Up Mitra

Stand-Up India applications and handholding.

www.startupindia.gov.in

Startup India

DPIIT recognition and startup benefits.

seedfund.startupindia.gov.in

Startup India Seed Fund

Seed grants and debt via incubators.

www.psbloansin59minutes.com

PSB Loans in 59 Minutes

In-principle MSME loan approvals.

pmfme.mofpi.gov.in

PMFME (MoFPI)

Micro food processing subsidy portal.

www.sidbi.in

SIDBI

Development bank for MSMEs.

www.nsic.co.in

NSIC

Tender registration, raw material and credit support.

msme.gov.in

Ministry of MSME

Official ministry website.

texmin.nic.in

Ministry of Textiles

Textile schemes including ATUFS.

gem.gov.in

GeM — Government e-Marketplace

Sell to government buyers.

Uttar Pradesh

msme1connect.up.gov.in

MSME 1-Connect (UP)

UP single portal for CM-YUVA, CMYSY and MSME schemes.

odopup.in

ODOP Uttar Pradesh

One District One Product programme.

invest.up.gov.in

Invest UP

Industrial investment policies and incentives.

niveshmitra.up.nic.in

Nivesh Mitra

UP single-window clearance system.

Gujarat

ic.gujarat.gov.in

Industries Commissionerate, Gujarat

Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026, 2022 Aatmanirbhar claims, Ambedkar Udhyog Uday.

blp.gujarat.gov.in

Bankable Loan Portal

Apply for Shri Vajpayee Bankable Yojana (loan up to ₹25 lakh with subsidy).

e-kutir.gujarat.gov.in

e-Kutir portal

Manav Kalyan Yojana toolkits and cottage-industry schemes.

mmuy.gujarat.gov.in

Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana

Interest-free loans for women’s groups.

esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in

e-Samaj Kalyan

SC welfare schemes, Manav Garima, GSCDC loans.

gueedc.gujarat.gov.in

GUEEDC

Self-employment loans for unreserved-category applicants.

gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in

GBCDC online

Loans for OBC / SEBC entrepreneurs.

adijatinigam.gujarat.gov.in

Gujarat Tribal Development Corporation

Loan schemes for Scheduled Tribe applicants.

www.ssipgujarat.in

SSIP Gujarat

Student startup and prototype grants.

ihubgujarat.in

i-Hub Gujarat

Startup Srujan Seed Support, testing and pilot grants.

dst.gujarat.gov.in

Department of Science & Technology

IT/ITeS, GCC and SpaceTech policies.

gsem.gujarat.gov.in

Gujarat State Electronics Mission

Electronics and semiconductor policies.

btm.gujarat.gov.in

Gujarat State Biotechnology Mission

Biotechnology Policy 2022-27.

ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in

iKhedut

Farm, horticulture, animal-husbandry and fisheries subsidies.

Maharashtra

maha-cmegp.gov.in

CMEGP portal

Chief Minister’s Employment Generation Programme applications.

Udyog MahaSwayam

MAITRI

Maharashtra investment facilitation and incentives.
