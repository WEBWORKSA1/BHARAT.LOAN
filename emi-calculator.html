How EMI is calculated

EMI on Indian bank loans uses the reducing-balance method — interest is charged only on the outstanding principal, which falls each month as you repay.

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)

Where P = principal, r = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = tenure in months.

Example: ₹10 Lakh at 11% over 5 years (60 months). Monthly rate = 11 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.00917. EMI = ₹21,742. You pay ₹13.04 Lakh in total — ₹10L principal + ₹3.04L interest.

How a subsidy reduces your EMI

A subsidy reduces the effective loan amount you have to repay. Smaller principal = smaller EMI, even at the same interest rate and tenure.

The most common subsidy in MSME lending is PMEGP's margin-money component — 15% to 35% of project cost depending on category, gender and location.

Worked example: ₹25 Lakh PMEGP manufacturing project, 25% subsidy, 11% rate, 5-yr tenure.

Without subsidy: EMI = ₹54,353/month, total payable ₹32.6L

With ₹6.25L subsidy: effective loan = ₹18.75L, EMI = ₹40,765/month, total payable ₹24.5L.

Lifetime saving: ₹8.15 Lakh.

EMI questions, answered

What interest rate should I assume for an Indian MSME loan?
Indian MSME loan rates typically range from 9% to 16% depending on lender, security, loan size and credit profile. CGTMSE-backed collateral-free loans usually carry rates of 10% to 13%. Public sector banks often quote lower rates than NBFCs. For an indicative calculation use 11% to 12% for a CGTMSE-eligible profile.
Does a PMEGP subsidy actually reduce my EMI?
Yes. PMEGP releases the subsidy as a margin-money grant adjusted against the loan after a lock-in period (typically 3 years). The bank disburses the full loan first; once subsidy criteria are met, your outstanding is reduced by the subsidy amount, which proportionally reduces your remaining EMIs — or your tenure if you keep the EMI constant.
Does CGTMSE change the interest rate?
CGTMSE does not directly change the EMI rate, but it unlocks loans for businesses that would otherwise be rejected for lack of collateral. The guarantee fee (1% to 2% annual on outstanding) is a separate cost not included in the standard EMI calculation. Some banks include it in the effective rate; some bill it separately.
Can I prepay an MSME loan without penalty?
Under RBI guidelines, banks cannot charge prepayment penalties on floating-rate loans for individual or MSE borrowers. Fixed-rate loans may carry a 2 to 4 percent prepayment penalty. Always confirm in the sanction letter before signing.
What's the difference between flat-rate and reducing-balance interest?
Reducing-balance interest is charged only on the outstanding principal — the standard for all Indian bank loans. Flat interest is charged on the full original principal throughout the tenure — usually quoted by some NBFCs and looks deceptively lower. A 12% flat rate is roughly equivalent to a 22% reducing-balance rate. This calculator uses reducing-balance.
What hidden costs aren't in this EMI?
Processing fee (typically 0.5% to 2% of loan, one-time), GST on processing fee, stamp duty on loan agreement (state-dependent), CGTMSE guarantee fee if applicable (1–2% annually), property insurance premium if secured, and CIBIL/credit report charges. Budget another 2–3% of loan amount as initial setup costs.
