PMEGP vs Mudra vs CGTMSE: which should you apply for first?

Three of India’s biggest MSME schemes, compared on what they give, how much, who qualifies — and how to stack them.

By the BHARAT.LOAN research desk · Reviewed by our advisory desk · Last reviewed 25 Nov 2026

Short answer: they do different jobs. PMEGP gives a subsidy for a new unit; Mudra is a collateral-free micro-loan up to ₹20 lakh for new or existing businesses; CGTMSE is a guarantee that lets banks lend up to ₹10 crore without collateral. PMEGP loans are often covered by CGTMSE, so they work together.

Which should you apply for first?

Starting a new manufacturing or service unit

Go for PMEGP first — the subsidy (15–35% of project cost, depending on category and urban/rural location) is money you keep once the unit completes its lock-in. Ask the bank to cover the loan under CGTMSE so you don’t need collateral. In Maharashtra compare with CMEGP; in Uttar Pradesh with CMYSY, ODOP and CM-YUVA — you can only use one self-employment subsidy for the same unit.

Running a small existing business

PMEGP won’t apply (it is for new units). A Mudra loan is the quickest collateral-free option up to ₹20 lakh.

Need more than ₹20 lakh, or working capital

Apply for a regular MSME term loan or cash credit and ask for CGTMSE cover. Check state interest subsidies (for example Aatmanirbhar Gujarat) that can reduce your interest on top.

Can you combine them?

  • PMEGP + CGTMSE: yes — common and recommended for larger PMEGP loans.
  • Mudra + CGTMSE-type cover: Mudra loans have their own guarantee framework; you don’t need to arrange it.
  • PMEGP + Mudra for the same unit: generally not — pick the one that fits.
  • PMEGP + state self-employment subsidy: no, not for the same unit.

Frequently asked questions

Is PMEGP better than Mudra?
For a brand-new unit, usually yes, because PMEGP includes a 15–35% subsidy you don’t repay. Mudra suits existing businesses and smaller needs, with simpler processing.
Can a PMEGP loan be collateral-free?
Loans up to ₹10 lakh under PMEGP generally don’t need collateral, and larger PMEGP loans are commonly covered under CGTMSE.
What is the maximum loan under CGTMSE?
Eligible micro and small enterprises can get collateral-free credit up to ₹10 crore under CGTMSE, subject to the lender’s appraisal.
Can I apply for PMEGP and Mudra together?
Not for the same unit. Choose the scheme that matches your stage — PMEGP for new units, Mudra for small existing ones.
