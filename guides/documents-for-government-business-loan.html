Documents for a government business loan: the complete checklist

Everything banks and nodal agencies usually ask for under Mudra, PMEGP, CGTMSE, Stand-Up India and state schemes — in one printable list.

By the BHARAT.LOAN research desk · Reviewed by our advisory desk · Last reviewed 25 Nov 2026

Print this page (use the Print / PDF button above) and tick items off as you collect them. Requirements vary by scheme and bank — this list covers what is asked most often.

1. Identity and address (promoters)

  • Aadhaar card (linked to your mobile number)
  • PAN card
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Address proof — Aadhaar, voter ID, passport or utility bill
  • Domicile certificate if a state scheme requires it

2. Business registration

  • Udyam Registration certificate (free at udyamregistration.gov.in)
  • GST registration (if applicable)
  • Partnership deed / LLP agreement / certificate of incorporation, MoA and AoA
  • Shop & Establishment licence or trade licence
  • Sector licences — e.g. FSSAI for food businesses

3. Project and finance

  • Detailed project report: activity, cost of project, means of finance, projected sales and costs, repayment schedule
  • Quotations for machinery and equipment (from authorised suppliers)
  • Proof of business premises — ownership or rent/lease agreement
  • Bank statements for the last 6–12 months
  • Income-tax returns for 2–3 years (existing businesses / individuals with income)
  • Audited or CA-certified financial statements (existing businesses)

4. Category and eligibility certificates

  • Caste certificate — SC/ST/OBC (for higher subsidy or targeted schemes)
  • Ex-servicemen or disability certificate (if applicable)
  • Educational qualification certificate (PMEGP/CMEGP for larger projects)
  • EDP / skill-training certificate (where required)
  • Income certificate (for income-linked schemes)

5. Scheme-specific extras

  • PMEGP / CMEGP: online application ID and acknowledgement; EDP training certificate before disbursement.
  • Stand-Up India: proof that it is a greenfield enterprise; shareholding pattern showing 51% SC/ST/woman control for companies.
  • Startups: DPIIT recognition certificate; pitch deck for the Seed Fund.
  • Machinery subsidies (ATUFS, CLCSS-type): machinery invoices and installation proof.
  • State incentives: land allotment, building plan and statutory approvals; eligibility certificate applications.

Tips that save weeks

  1. Make names and addresses identical across Aadhaar, PAN, bank and Udyam.
  2. Scan documents clearly within the portal’s file-size limits.
  3. Keep a single folder with acknowledgement numbers for every submission.
  4. Never hand original documents to an agent.

Frequently asked questions

What documents are needed for a Mudra loan?
Typically identity and address proof, business proof such as Udyam registration, quotations for items to be purchased, recent bank statements and, for larger Tarun loans, financial statements and a project report.
Is Udyam registration mandatory for government loans?
Most MSME schemes and many bank scheme loans ask for Udyam registration. It is free and quick, so register before you apply.
Do I need a project report for a small loan?
For PMEGP, CMEGP and similar subsidy schemes, yes. For small Mudra loans banks may accept a simple business plan, but a clear project report always improves your chances.
Can an agent submit documents for me?
Only you should submit on official portals or at the bank. Never hand over original documents or OTPs to agents.
