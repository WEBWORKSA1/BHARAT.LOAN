What we do

There are more than 50 central and state government schemes paying out subsidies, capital grants, interest subvention and collateral-free credit to Indian MSMEs and startups. Most business owners don't know which they qualify for. Fewer still file successfully. The application machinery is opaque, fragmented and intimidating — and the schemes change all the time.

BHARAT.LOAN exists to close that gap. We continuously verify the scheme reference, run an eligibility assessment in four questions, and — if you authorise it — take the application all the way through approval and disbursement.

Our founding advisor

The advisory desk is led by a 25-year finance professional with deep operating experience in MSME credit, government scheme structuring and subsidy disbursement. Engagements span central schemes (CGTMSE, PMEGP, CLCSS, Mudra, Startup India Seed Fund, DPIIT 80-IAC), Gujarat state schemes (Aatmanirbhar Gujarat, textile policy, large industry incentive, startup scheme) and Uttar Pradesh schemes (CM-YUVA, CMYSY, ODOP across all 75 districts, UPIEPP 2022, MSME policy, textile policy).

How we deliver

  • Assess — review your business profile, sector, stage and location to map every scheme you qualify for.
  • Strategise — rank schemes by value and likelihood, designing your optimal funding stack.
  • File — prepare and submit applications end-to-end with full documentation and compliance.
  • Disburse — liaise with banks and nodal agencies through approval and disbursement.

Advisory engagements are delivered in partnership with CONSULT.IN, an independent advisory firm.

Our coverage

Central schemes are nationwide. State depth currently covers Gujarat (4 schemes) and Uttar Pradesh (6 schemes plus all 75 districts under the One District One Product programme). Maharashtra and other industrial states are rolling out.

What we do not do

We are not a government body. We do not own or operate any scheme. We are not a bank or NBFC and do not lend money. We cannot guarantee approval — every scheme approval is at the discretion of the relevant authority. What we do is dramatically improve the quality and completeness of your application, and route you to the schemes that actually fit your business.
