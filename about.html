What we do

BHARAT.LOAN is a continuously-verified reference for every major central and state government scheme, subsidy and incentive available to Indian startups, MSMEs and industrial units. We cover credit guarantees, capital subsidies, interest subventions, net SGST reimbursement, seed funding, tax exemptions and sector-specific incentives across central schemes and the states we are live in.

Once you know what you qualify for, our advisory partner CONSULT.IN takes over and handles the application end-to-end — eligibility confirmation, project report preparation, document collation, filing, bank or nodal-agency liaison, and disbursement follow-up.

The advisor

Advisory delivery is led by a finance professional with a 25-year career across Indian banking, MSME credit and corporate advisory. The depth comes from sitting on the lender side, the borrower side and the consultant side over the years — knowing which schemes actually disburse, which gatekeepers matter at each District Industries Centre, and where the bottlenecks are between in-principle approval and money in the account.

That experience is the difference between a portal application that sits in a queue and a file that lands on the right desk with the right framing.

How we work

  1. Assess. We review your business profile — sector, stage, state, district, project size, employment plan — and map every scheme you qualify for.
  2. Strategise. We rank the matches by net value and likelihood, factoring in stacking limits, anti-double-dipping rules and timing windows, and design your optimal funding stack.
  3. File. We prepare and submit applications end-to-end, with the project report, documentation, statutory approvals and compliance trail each scheme requires.
  4. Disburse. We liaise with banks, nodal agencies and DICs through sanction, approval and disbursement — and through the subsidy claim cycle for reimbursement schemes.
