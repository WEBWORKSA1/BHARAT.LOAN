Cookie Policy

The cookies and local storage this website uses, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. We also use “local storage” in your browser for simple preferences. Some are set by us and some by third parties.

Cookies we use

  • Essential / preferences (ours): remember that you dismissed the cookie notice, your chosen language, your first-visit source (for attribution of enquiries) and any form that could not be sent so it can retry. No personal data is shared through these.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity — when enabled): measure visits and how pages are used so we can improve them. IP addresses are anonymised.
  • Advertising (Google AdSense): Google and its partners use cookies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Manage personalisation at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Embedded video (YouTube, privacy-enhanced mode): YouTube may set cookies only after you click play.
  • Translation (Google Translate): a “googtrans” cookie stores the language you select.

Consent for EEA/UK visitors

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland see a Google consent message and can accept or refuse personalised advertising.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect features such as remembering your language. You can also use your browser’s private mode.

Questions? See our Privacy Policy or use the contact form.

Get new schemes & deadlines first

One short email when something changes — new schemes, revised limits, application windows. No spam.
