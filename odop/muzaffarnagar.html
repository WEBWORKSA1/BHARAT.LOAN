Muzaffarnagar ODOP at a glance

If you run or plan a unit making jaggery (gur) in Muzaffarnagar district, you qualify to apply for ODOP margin-money support of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh. No educational-qualification bar; applicant must be 18+.

What you can get
Margin-money financial grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs for larger projects (up to ₹50 lakh and ₹150 lakh per the scheme guidelines). The unit must make Muzaffarnagar's designated ODOP product: jaggery (gur).
ODOP was launched in 2018 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to revive each district's indigenous product. The margin-money grant is routed through the lending bank and coordinates with the CM self-employment schemes (CMYSY / CM-YUVA) and Vishwakarma Shram Samman for the financing flow. It cannot be combined with PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit.

Who it's for

Entrepreneurs, artisans and micro-units in Muzaffarnagar who manufacture, service or trade in the district's designated ODOP product — jaggery (gur). Muzaffarnagar sits in UP's sugarcane heartland and is one of India's largest jaggery (gur) producing and trading centres, with a vast network of khandsari and gur-processing units.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Muzaffarnagar's designated ODOP product: jaggery (gur)
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another central/state self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Muzaffarnagar

  1. Confirm your unit makes Muzaffarnagar's designated ODOP product (jaggery / gur)
  2. Prepare a project report for the product
  3. Apply through the ODOP margin-money channel via the Muzaffarnagar District Industries Centre (often routed through CMYSY)
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan
