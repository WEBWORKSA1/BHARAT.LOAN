ODOP Saharanpur: Wood carving

One District One Product · Saharanpur district · Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP

Saharanpur's designated ODOP product is wood carving. Saharanpur is renowned for GI-tagged intricate wood carving in sheesham, supplying carved furniture, panels and decorative items to domestic and export markets.

What you can get
Margin-money grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs above (₹50L and ₹150L per scheme guidelines). The unit must make Saharanpur's designated ODOP product: wood carving.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Saharanpur's designated ODOP product: wood carving
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Saharanpur

  1. Confirm your unit makes Saharanpur's designated ODOP product
  2. Prepare a project report
  3. Apply via the Saharanpur District Industries Centre
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

Documents you'll need

Aadhaar and KYCProof of Saharanpur residence / unit locationProject reportBank account detailsCaste / category certificate (if applicable)
