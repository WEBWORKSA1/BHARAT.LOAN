ODOP Moradabad: Metal craft (brassware)

One District One Product · Moradabad district · Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP

Moradabad's designated ODOP product is metal craft (brassware). Moradabad is 'Peetal Nagri' (Brass City), India's leading brass-handicraft export cluster with GI-tagged metal craft, exporting decorative and utility brassware worldwide.

What you can get
Margin-money grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs above (₹50L and ₹150L per scheme guidelines). The unit must make Moradabad's designated ODOP product: brass metal craft.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Moradabad's designated ODOP product: brass metal craft
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Moradabad

  1. Confirm your unit makes Moradabad's designated ODOP product
  2. Prepare a project report
  3. Apply via the Moradabad District Industries Centre
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

Aadhaar and KYCProof of Moradabad residence / unit locationProject reportBank account detailsCaste / category certificate (if applicable)
