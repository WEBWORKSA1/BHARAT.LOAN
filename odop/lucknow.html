ODOP Lucknow: Chikankari & zari-zardozi

One District One Product · Lucknow district · Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP

Lucknow's designated ODOP product is Chikankari & zari-zardozi. Lucknow is the home of GI-tagged Chikankari, the delicate hand-embroidery tradition, alongside zardozi metallic-thread work; the craft sustains a very large, predominantly women, artisan base.

What you can get
Margin-money grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs above (₹50L and ₹150L per scheme guidelines). The unit must make Lucknow's designated ODOP product: Chikankari or zari-zardozi.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Lucknow's designated ODOP product: Chikankari or zari-zardozi
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Lucknow

  1. Confirm your unit makes Lucknow's designated ODOP product
  2. Prepare a project report
  3. Apply via the Lucknow District Industries Centre
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

Documents you'll need

Aadhaar and KYCProof of Lucknow residence / unit locationProject reportBank account detailsCaste / category certificate (if applicable)
