Kanpur Dehat ODOP at a glance

If you run or plan a unit making aluminium utensils in Kanpur Dehat district, you qualify to apply for ODOP margin-money support of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh. No educational-qualification bar; applicant must be 18+.

What you can get
Margin-money financial grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs for larger projects (up to ₹50 lakh and ₹150 lakh per the scheme guidelines). The unit must make Kanpur Dehat's designated ODOP product: aluminium utensils.
ODOP was launched in 2018 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to revive each district's indigenous product. The margin-money grant is routed through the lending bank and coordinates with the CM self-employment schemes (CMYSY / CM-YUVA) and Vishwakarma Shram Samman for the financing flow. It cannot be combined with PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit.

Who it's for

Entrepreneurs, artisans and micro-units in Kanpur Dehat who manufacture, service or trade in the district's designated ODOP product — aluminium utensils. Kanpur Dehat hosts a cluster of aluminium utensil and cookware units supplying household and commercial kitchenware, building on the greater Kanpur industrial belt's metalworking base.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Kanpur Dehat's designated ODOP product: aluminium utensils
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another central/state self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Kanpur Dehat

  1. Confirm your unit makes Kanpur Dehat's designated ODOP product (aluminium utensils)
  2. Prepare a project report for the product
  3. Apply through the ODOP margin-money channel via the Kanpur Dehat District Industries Centre (often routed through CMYSY)
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

How much margin money can I get in Kanpur Dehat?
Up to 25% of project cost capped at ₹6.25 lakh for projects up to ₹25 lakh, with tiered slabs above that. Category applicants may qualify for an enhanced rate.
