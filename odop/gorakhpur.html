ODOP Gorakhpur: Terracotta & readymade garments

One District One Product · Gorakhpur district · Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP

Gorakhpur's designated ODOP product is terracotta & readymade garments. Gorakhpur is famed for GI-tagged Gorakhpur terracotta — the painted clay figures and pottery of Aurangabad village — a heritage potter craft, with a readymade-garment base as a second track.

What you can get
Margin-money grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs above (₹50L and ₹150L per scheme guidelines). The unit must make Gorakhpur's designated ODOP product: terracotta or readymade garments.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Gorakhpur's designated ODOP product: terracotta or readymade garments
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Gorakhpur

  1. Confirm your unit makes Gorakhpur's designated ODOP product
  2. Prepare a project report
  3. Apply via the Gorakhpur District Industries Centre
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

Aadhaar and KYCProof of Gorakhpur residence / unit locationProject reportBank account detailsCaste / category certificate (if applicable)

Frequently asked

What is Gorakhpur's ODOP product?
Gorakhpur's designated ODOP is GI-tagged Gorakhpur terracotta plus readymade garments. The terracotta cluster is centred at Aurangabad village.
How much margin money can I get in Gorakhpur?
Up to 25% of project cost capped at ₹6.25 lakh for projects up to ₹25 lakh.
Can I combine ODOP with PMEGP in Gorakhpur?
Generally not for the same unit.
