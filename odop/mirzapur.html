ODOP Mirzapur: Carpets & brass industry

One District One Product · Mirzapur district · Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP

Mirzapur's designated ODOP product is carpets & brass industry. Mirzapur, part of the historic carpet belt alongside Bhadohi, is a major hand-knotted carpet and dhurrie weaving cluster with deep export linkages, plus an allied brass industry.

What you can get
Margin-money grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs above (₹50L and ₹150L per scheme guidelines). The unit must make Mirzapur's designated ODOP product: carpets or brass.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Mirzapur's designated ODOP product: carpets or brass
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Mirzapur

  1. Confirm your unit makes Mirzapur's designated ODOP product
  2. Prepare a project report
  3. Apply via the Mirzapur District Industries Centre
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

Documents you'll need

Aadhaar and KYCProof of Mirzapur residence / unit locationProject reportBank account detailsCaste / category certificate (if applicable)
