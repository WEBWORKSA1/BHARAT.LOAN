Rampur ODOP at a glance

If you run or plan a unit making applique & patchwork, zari patchwork, or mentha products in Rampur district, you qualify to apply for ODOP margin-money support of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh. No educational-qualification bar; applicant must be 18+.

What you can get
Margin-money financial grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs for larger projects (up to ₹50 lakh and ₹150 lakh per the scheme guidelines). The unit must make Rampur's designated ODOP product: applique & patchwork, zari patchwork, or mentha.
ODOP was launched in 2018 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to revive each district's indigenous product. The margin-money grant is routed through the lending bank and coordinates with the CM self-employment schemes (CMYSY / CM-YUVA) and Vishwakarma Shram Samman for the financing flow. It cannot be combined with PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit.

Who it's for

Entrepreneurs, artisans and micro-units in Rampur who manufacture, service or trade in the district's designated ODOP product — applique & patchwork, zari patchwork, mentha. Rampur is known for applique and patchwork and zari-patchwork embroidery, alongside being a major mentha (mint) producing and processing belt.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Rampur's designated ODOP product: applique & patchwork, zari patchwork, mentha
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another central/state self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Rampur

  1. Confirm your unit makes Rampur's designated ODOP product (applique & patchwork, zari patchwork, or mentha)
  2. Prepare a project report for the product
  3. Apply through the ODOP margin-money channel via the Rampur District Industries Centre (often routed through CMYSY)
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

What is Rampur's ODOP product?

How much margin money can I get in Rampur?
Up to 25% of project cost capped at ₹6.25 lakh for projects up to ₹25 lakh, with tiered slabs above that. Category applicants may qualify for an enhanced rate.
Can I combine ODOP with PMEGP in Rampur?
