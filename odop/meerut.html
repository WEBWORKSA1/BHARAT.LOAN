ODOP Meerut: Sports products

One District One Product · Meerut district · Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP

Meerut's designated ODOP product is sports products. Meerut is one of India's principal sports-goods manufacturing hubs, producing cricket bats, balls and a wide range of sporting equipment for national and international markets.

What you can get
Margin-money grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs above (₹50L and ₹150L per scheme guidelines). The unit must make Meerut's designated ODOP product: sports products.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Meerut's designated ODOP product: sports products
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Meerut

  1. Confirm your unit makes Meerut's designated ODOP product
  2. Prepare a project report
  3. Apply via the Meerut District Industries Centre
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

Documents you'll need

Aadhaar and KYCProof of Meerut residence / unit locationProject reportBank account detailsCaste / category certificate (if applicable)
