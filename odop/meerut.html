Meerut ODOP at a glance

If you run or plan a unit making sports products in Meerut district, you qualify to apply for ODOP margin-money support of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh. No educational-qualification bar; applicant must be 18+.

What you can get
Margin-money financial grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs for larger projects (up to ₹50 lakh and ₹150 lakh per the scheme guidelines). The unit must make Meerut's designated ODOP product: sports products.
ODOP was launched in 2018 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to revive each district's indigenous product. The margin-money grant is routed through the lending bank and coordinates with the CM self-employment schemes (CMYSY / CM-YUVA) and Vishwakarma Shram Samman for the financing flow. It cannot be combined with PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit.

Who it's for

Entrepreneurs, artisans and micro-units in Meerut who manufacture, service or trade in the district's designated ODOP product — sports products. Meerut is one of India's principal sports-goods manufacturing hubs, producing cricket bats, balls and a wide range of sporting equipment for national and international markets.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Meerut's designated ODOP product: sports products
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another central/state self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Meerut

  1. Confirm your unit makes Meerut's designated ODOP product (sports products)
  2. Prepare a project report for the product
  3. Apply through the ODOP margin-money channel via the Meerut District Industries Centre (often routed through CMYSY)
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan

Frequently asked

What is Meerut's ODOP product?
Meerut's designated One District One Product is sports products. To qualify for ODOP-specific support your unit must operate in this product category.
How much margin money can I get in Meerut?
Up to 25% of project cost capped at ₹6.25 lakh for projects up to ₹25 lakh, with tiered slabs above that. Category applicants may qualify for an enhanced rate.
Can I combine ODOP with PMEGP in Meerut?
Generally not for the same unit — you can't double-dip across self-employment schemes. The right single route (ODOP vs CMYSY vs CM-YUVA vs PMEGP) depends on your project size and category. We model it for you.
