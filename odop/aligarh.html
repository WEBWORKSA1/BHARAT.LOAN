ODOP Aligarh: Locks & hardware, metal handicraft

One District One Product · Aligarh district · Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP

Aligarh's designated ODOP product is locks & hardware, metal handicraft. Aligarh is India's lock-making capital, producing the bulk of the country's locks and builder hardware through a very large base of small and micro units, with allied metal-handicraft work.

What you can get
Margin-money grant of 25% of project cost, up to ₹6.25 lakh, for projects up to ₹25 lakh — with higher tiered slabs above (₹50L and ₹150L per scheme guidelines). The unit must make Aligarh's designated ODOP product: locks & hardware, metal handicraft.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in Aligarh's designated ODOP product: locks & hardware, metal handicraft
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank, financial or government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP or another self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply in Aligarh

  1. Confirm your unit makes Aligarh's designated ODOP product
  2. Prepare a project report for the product
  3. Apply through the ODOP margin-money channel via the Aligarh District Industries Centre
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; the margin-money grant is credited against the loan
