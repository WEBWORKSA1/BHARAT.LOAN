Questions about the matcher

How accurate is the matcher?
It applies each scheme’s main published conditions — location, business stage, sector, project size and applicant category. Final eligibility always depends on the official guidelines and the bank or nodal agency, which our advisor confirms with you.
Is this really free?
Yes. The matcher and the first assessment call are free. If you later want us to prepare and file your applications, the scope and any fee are agreed with you in writing before work starts.
Will you share my details?
Your details go only to our advisory desk to respond to your request. We do not sell your data. See our Privacy Policy.
Can I combine several schemes?
Often yes — for example a state interest subsidy with a CGTMSE-backed loan. But self-employment subsidies like PMEGP, CMEGP and state margin-money schemes usually can’t be combined for the same unit. Your plan shows which ones stack.

Get new schemes & deadlines first

One short email when something changes — new schemes, revised limits, application windows. No spam.
