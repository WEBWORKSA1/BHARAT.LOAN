FAQ

Common questions

Is BHARAT.LOAN a government website?
No. BHARAT.LOAN is an independent guide and advisory publisher. We are not affiliated with any ministry, department or bank. We explain schemes in plain language, link to the official portals and help you apply correctly.
Do I have to pay to apply for a government scheme?
No. Applying for government schemes on official portals is free. Our first eligibility assessment is also free. Be careful of anyone who asks for money to ‘guarantee’ a loan or subsidy approval.
Which schemes can a new business get without collateral?
Mudra loans (up to ₹20 lakh), CGTMSE-backed bank loans (up to ₹10 crore), PMEGP and CMEGP margin-money loans, Stand-Up India for SC/ST and women founders, and UP’s CM-YUVA (0% interest up to ₹5 lakh) are the main collateral-free routes.
How often is the information updated?
Scheme news and video sections refresh automatically every day. Scheme pages show the date they were last reviewed, and we revise them whenever limits or rules change. Always confirm the latest terms on the official portal before you apply.
What happens after I submit my details?
An advisor from our desk reviews your profile and contacts you within one business day with the schemes you qualify for and the next steps. There is no obligation.
