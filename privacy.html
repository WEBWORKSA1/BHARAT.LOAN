Privacy Policy

How BHARAT.LOAN collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 5 June 2026 · Governed by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)

1. Who we are

BHARAT.LOAN is an independent advisory service that maps Indian government schemes, subsidies and incentives for businesses. Advisory delivery is handled by our partner CONSULT.IN. For the purpose of the DPDPA 2023 we act as the Data Fiduciary for personal data collected through this website.

You can reach us at webworksa1@gmail.com for any privacy-related query.

2. What data we collect

Information you give us directly

When you fill in any consultation request, eligibility check or contact form on this site we collect: your name, business or company name, phone number (including WhatsApp number where applicable), email address, business location (state or district), indicative loan or project size, and any free-text notes you choose to share with us. These fields are clearly visible at the point of collection.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics: via Google Analytics 4 — anonymised IP address, pages viewed, time on site, device and browser type, referring URL.
  • Session insights: via Microsoft Clarity — anonymised session recordings and heatmaps to understand how the site is used. Sensitive form inputs are masked by default.
  • Advertising: via Google AdSense — cookies that support ad serving and measurement. You can adjust ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider records standard request logs (IP, user-agent, timestamp) for security and reliability.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your consultation, eligibility or contact request — typically by phone, WhatsApp or email within one business day.
  • To assess which government schemes and incentives your business may qualify for.
  • To deliver advisory services through our partner CONSULT.IN if you choose to engage.
  • To improve the website's content, structure and performance.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and accounting obligations.
We do not sell your personal data. We do not share lead data with banks, lenders or third-party advertisers without your express consent.

4. Who we share data with

  • CONSULT.IN: our advisory partner — receives the lead and engagement data necessary to deliver the consultation.
  • FormSubmit: the form-relay service that delivers consultation requests to our inbox.
  • Google (Analytics + AdSense): aggregate, anonymised analytics and ad-serving data.
  • Microsoft (Clarity): aggregated session-behaviour data.
  • Authorities: where required by Indian law, regulation or a lawful order.

5. Your rights under the DPDPA

As a Data Principal under India's DPDPA 2023 you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data we hold.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent previously given (this will not affect lawful processing already completed).
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights in case of incapacity or death.
  • Raise a grievance with our Data Fiduciary contact.

To exercise any of these rights, email webworksa1@gmail.com with the subject line "DPDPA Request". We will respond within the timelines required by law.

6. Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies to operate, analyse usage and serve ads. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings; doing so may degrade some site features. AdSense ad personalisation can be controlled at myadcenter.google.com.

7. Data retention

  • Lead and consultation records: up to 24 months from your last interaction, then erased or anonymised.
  • Analytics data (Google Analytics): standard 14-month retention.
  • Records required by tax, accounting or other law: retained for the statutory period.

8. Children

This site is intended for adult business owners, founders and finance professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has shared data with us, please email us and we will erase it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data including HTTPS in transit, restricted advisor access, and timely deletion of data no longer needed. No online service is perfectly secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage for a reasonable period.

11. Grievance & contact

For privacy-related queries, requests or grievances, please email webworksa1@gmail.com. We aim to respond within one business day and to resolve grievances within the timelines prescribed by the DPDPA.
