Privacy Policy

How BHARAT.LOAN collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Who we are

BHARAT.LOAN (“we”, “us”) is an independent information publisher and advisory service covering Indian government schemes, subsidies and incentives for businesses. Advisory delivery may be handled by our advisory partner. For the purposes of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) we act as the Data Fiduciary for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us for any privacy matter through our contact form (choose “Privacy / data request”).

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you use a consultation, eligibility, newsletter, contest, careers, support or partnership form we collect the details you enter — for example your name, phone number, email, state, city or district, business details, funding need, and any message. Optional fields are marked or are clearly optional.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data such as pages viewed, referring page, approximate location derived from IP, browser and device type — via Google Analytics 4 (with IP anonymisation) and, if enabled, Microsoft Clarity session insights (sensitive inputs masked).
  • Advertising data — see section 6.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability.
  • Local storage in your browser to remember choices such as your language, cookie notice and first visit source.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your request and provide the eligibility assessment or advisory service you asked for.
  • To send the newsletter or alerts you subscribed to (you can unsubscribe any time).
  • To run contests, process job applications and handle support pledges.
  • To improve our content, tools and site performance, and to keep the site secure.
  • To show advertising that keeps this resource free (section 6).
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your enquiry with banks, lenders or other third parties without your consent.

4. Legal basis and consent

We process the personal data you submit on the basis of your consent, given through the consent checkbox on each form, and for the legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act. You can withdraw consent at any time through our contact form; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

5. Who we share data with

  • Our advisory partner, only to respond to an advisory request you made.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf: form delivery (FormSubmit), hosting (GitHub Pages), analytics (Google, Microsoft) and email.
  • Authorities, where required by law or a lawful order.

6. Advertising, Google AdSense and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show advertisements. In connection with this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings (also available at My Ad Center). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message powered by Google’s consent management platform before personalised ads are served.

More detail is in our Cookie Policy.

7. Embedded content and links

Videos on this site are embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode; YouTube may set cookies only once you play a video. Language translation uses Google Translate when you choose a language. News headlines and official-portal links take you to third-party websites governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may: access a summary of the personal data we hold about you; ask us to correct, complete or update it; ask us to erase it (subject to legal retention); withdraw consent; nominate another person to exercise your rights; and raise a grievance. Use our contact form and select “Privacy / data request”. We respond within the timelines required by law.

9. Retention

  • Enquiry and consultation records: up to 24 months after your last interaction, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months.
  • Analytics: per the provider’s retention settings (typically 14 months).
  • Records we must keep by law: for the statutory period.

10. Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and contests are open only to adults. If you believe a minor has shared data with us, tell us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use HTTPS, restrict access to enquiry data and delete data we no longer need. No online service can be perfectly secure.

12. Grievance officer

For complaints about how we handle personal data, contact our Grievance Officer through the contact form (topic: “Privacy / data request” or “Grievance”). If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational for your matter.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top always shows the current version.
