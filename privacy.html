Privacy Policy

How BHARAT.LOAN collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information.

1. Who we are

BHARAT.LOAN (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent government scheme and subsidy advisory service for Indian businesses, operated as part of the Webworks Media Network. Advisory engagements are delivered in partnership with CONSULT.IN. We are not a government body and do not represent any ministry or agency.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share your personal information when you visit bharat.loan or submit a consultation request. It is aligned with the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

2. Information we collect

When you submit our eligibility engine, consultation form, or contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number and/or WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Business or company name (where provided)
  • Business stage, sector, state and funding need (where provided)
  • Indicative loan or project size (where provided)

We also automatically collect technical information when you visit the site, including IP address and approximate location, browser and device type, pages visited, time on page, and referring URL.

3. How we use your information

Your personal information is used only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your consultation request and provide a scheme-eligibility assessment
  • To structure and file scheme applications that you authorise us to handle
  • To communicate with you about your engagement (by phone, WhatsApp or email)
  • To improve our website, content and services
  • To comply with applicable Indian law

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

4. Third parties and processors

We use the following third-party services that may process limited data on our behalf:

  • FormSubmit — lead form delivery to our advisory desk
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymised website analytics
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymised session recordings and heatmaps used to improve UX
  • Google AdSense — advertising; may use cookies for personalisation
  • CONSULT.IN — advisory delivery partner for engagements you authorise

Each of these operates under its own privacy policy.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential site functionality, analytics (Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity — anonymised by default) and advertising personalisation (Google AdSense). You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features may be limited.

6. Data retention

We retain your lead and engagement information for as long as needed to deliver advisory services and to comply with applicable laws. Analytics data is retained per Google and Microsoft default retention periods (typically 14–26 months).

7. Your rights under DPDPA

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to request correction or update of inaccurate information, to request erasure of your data (subject to legal retention requirements), to withdraw consent for ongoing communications, and to lodge a grievance with our designated Data Officer.

To exercise any of these rights, email webworksa1@gmail.com with the subject “DPDPA Request” and your name and phone used to contact us.

8. Children

BHARAT.LOAN is a B2B advisory service and is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date below reflects the current version.

10. Contact

For any privacy questions or DPDPA requests, write to webworksa1@gmail.com.
