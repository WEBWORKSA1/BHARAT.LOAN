How subsidies are calculated

Most Indian government subsidies fall into three families: margin-money grants (PMEGP, ODOP UP) — a % of project cost adjusted against your bank loan after a lock-in period; capital subsidies (CLCSS, state schemes) — a % of plant and machinery cost paid out after commissioning; and interest subventions (Aatmanirbhar Gujarat) — the government pays part of your loan interest each year.

The estimator computes the rupee value of each based on scheme rules currently published. State-level interest subventions are quantified as a one-time-equivalent assuming a 5-year loan tenure for comparability.

PMEGP rate logic:
• Urban · General: 15% of project cost
• Urban · Special category: 25%
• Rural · General: 25%
• Rural · Special category: 35%

"Special category" = women, SC/ST, OBC, minorities, ex-servicemen, differently-abled, NER.

Why stacking rules matter

If you naively sum every scheme you match for, you'll dramatically overstate your subsidy. The big anti-double-dipping rules in current MSME funding:

  • PMEGP cannot be combined with CLCSS or with ODOP-UP margin money for the same unit.
  • CLCSS is upgrade-only — for new units choose PMEGP or a state new-unit subsidy instead.
  • State capital subsidies typically stack with CGTMSE (which is a guarantee, not a subsidy) and with central interest subventions, but not with central capital subsidies for the same fixed-asset line item.
  • SISFS (Startup Seed Fund) is for DPIIT-recognised startups only and follows separate rules.
Worked example: ₹20L manufacturing project, new unit, rural, general category, in UP. Matched: PMEGP rural-general at 25% = ₹5L; ODOP-UP at 25% = ₹5L; UP MSME Policy capital subsidy at 15% = ₹3L.

PMEGP and ODOP-UP both target margin money for the same unit — you pick the higher. PMEGP wins at ₹5L. UP MSME Policy capital subsidy is on a different line item and may stack — net stackable ₹8L (~40%).

Subsidy questions, answered

Are these estimates guaranteed amounts?
No. These are indicative estimates based on scheme rules as currently published. Actual subsidy approval depends on document verification, budget availability with the disbursing agency, project viability assessment by the lending bank, and category-specific guidelines that change from time to time. Always confirm with the relevant authority before acting.
Can I really claim multiple subsidies for the same project?
Some can stack, some cannot. PMEGP and CLCSS cannot be combined for the same unit. PMEGP and ODOP UP cannot be combined for the same unit. State-level capital subsidy and central CLCSS can sometimes stack on different line items. The estimator applies the main anti-double-dipping rules; an advisor confirms the legal maximum stack for your specific case.
How is the PMEGP subsidy actually disbursed?
PMEGP releases the margin-money subsidy as a grant adjusted against the bank loan after a lock-in period of three years, provided the unit remains operational and EDP training is completed. The bank initially disburses the full loan; the subsidy reduces the outstanding after lock-in.
Does CLCSS apply to my industry?
CLCSS applies to 51 notified manufacturing sub-sectors including bio-tech, drugs, food processing, textiles, glass and ceramics, plastics, leather and footwear, and many engineering categories. It is upgrade-only — the subsidy is on plant and machinery for technology upgrade, not new units. Check the latest 51-sector list on the Ministry of MSME site.
How long does subsidy approval take?
Timelines vary by scheme and state. PMEGP typically 45 to 120 days from application to in-principle approval, plus the three-year lock-in for subsidy adjustment. CLCSS 90 to 180 days. State capital subsidies often 60 to 180 days from production start. Realistic plan: budget 6 to 12 months from project filing to subsidy actually adjusted.
What documents do I need to claim these subsidies?
Typical core set: Udyam registration, GST registration, PAN, Aadhaar of all promoters, project report with technical and financial details, bank loan sanction letter, land or premises proof, NOCs as applicable, EDP training certificate for PMEGP, and category proof certificates where claiming special-category benefits. Scheme-specific docs are detailed on each scheme page on this site.
