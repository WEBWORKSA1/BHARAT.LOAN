Official contest rules (summary)

  1. Skill-based only. Every BHARAT.LOAN contest is decided on skill, knowledge or merit judged against published criteria. There is no lottery, draw of lots or element of chance, and no purchase or payment is ever required to enter.
  2. Eligibility. Open to residents of India aged 18 or above, unless a contest page says otherwise. Employees and partners of BHARAT.LOAN and their immediate families are not eligible for prizes.
  3. Entries. One entry per person per round unless stated. Entries must be your own original work, truthful, and must not infringe anyone’s rights. We may reject entries that are offensive, plagiarised or misleading.
  4. Judging. Entries are judged by a BHARAT.LOAN panel (and sponsor representatives where applicable) on the criteria announced for each round. The panel’s decision is final.
  5. Prizes. Prizes are as announced on the contest page when the round opens, are non-transferable, and may be subject to applicable taxes (including TDS where required by law). Winners must provide valid ID and payment details to receive cash prizes.
  6. Publicity. By entering, winners agree that their name, city and entry (or an edited version) may be published on BHARAT.LOAN and its channels. You may ask to be featured by first name only.
  7. Privacy. Entry details are used only to run the contest and contact you, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  8. Changes. We may modify, suspend or cancel a contest if required for legal or operational reasons, and will post any change on this page.
