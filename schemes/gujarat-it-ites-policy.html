Gujarat IT/ITeS Policy 2022-27: 25% CAPEX + 15% OPEX support

Gujarat IT/ITeS Policy 2022-27 · Department of Science & Technology, Gujarat

For IT and IT-enabled services companies in Gujarat — 25% of capital expenditure and 15% of operating expenditure reimbursed, plus hiring bonuses, EPF support and a 7% interest subsidy.

CAPEX
25%, max ₹50–200 Cr
OPEX
15% for 5 yrs

Quick eligibility read

New or expanding IT/ITeS unit in Gujarat — software, BPO/KPO, data centres, IT parks — investing in eligible capex and creating local jobs.

What you can get
25% CAPEX support (up to ₹50 crore, or ₹200 crore for mega projects) and 15% OPEX support for 5 years (up to ₹20–40 crore a year).
Plus one-time hiring incentive, EPF reimbursement, 7% interest subsidy and electricity-duty reimbursement.

Incentive menu

IncentiveCategory I (investment below ₹250 Cr)Category II — mega (₹250 Cr+ or 2,000+ IT jobs)
CAPEX support25% of eligible capex, max ₹50 Cr (20 quarterly instalments)25%, max ₹200 Cr
OPEX support15% of eligible opex for 5 years, max ₹20 Cr a year15%, max ₹40 Cr a year
  • Interest subsidy: 7% (or actual) on term loans, up to ₹1 crore a year for 5 years
  • Employment generation incentive: 50% of one month’s CTC per new local hire — up to ₹50,000 (men) and ₹60,000 (women)
  • EPF reimbursement: 100% (women) / 75% (men) of the employer contribution for 5 years
  • Electricity duty: 100% reimbursement for 5 years
  • Rent support (empanelled spaces): 50% up to ₹10,000 per seat per month in years 1–2, then 25% up to ₹5,000
  • Data centres: 25% capex up to ₹150 Cr plus ₹1/unit power subsidy for 5 years; cable landing stations 25% up to ₹20 Cr
  • R&D labs at institutes: 60% of equipment cost up to ₹5 crore

The first three projects of ₹100 crore or more qualify as mega projects (early-mover). A High-Powered Committee can enhance incentives.

Who it’s for

Software companies, IT-enabled services, data centres, IT townships and cable landing stations setting up or expanding in Gujarat.

Eligibility

  • IT/ITeS unit located in Gujarat
  • New unit or expansion with eligible capex
  • Category I below ₹250 crore investment; mega at ₹250 crore+ or 2,000+ IT employees
  • New employees for hiring and EPF incentives
  • Term loan from an RBI-regulated institution for interest subsidy (NBFCs excluded)

How to apply

  1. Register the project with the Department of Science & Technology
  2. Get in-principle approval
  3. Invest and start operations
  4. Claim CAPEX in quarterly instalments and OPEX annually
  5. Claim hiring and EPF incentives for eligible new employees

Documents you’ll need

Company incorporation documentsProject reportCA certificate of capexLease / ownership papersEmployee payroll and EPF recordsTerm-loan documents

Frequently asked

What does the Gujarat IT/ITeS Policy 2022-27 offer?
A CAPEX-OPEX model: 25% of capital expenditure (up to ₹50 crore, or ₹200 crore for mega projects) and 15% of operating expenditure for five years (up to ₹20 crore a year, or ₹40 crore), plus hiring, EPF, interest and electricity-duty support.
Is it only for big companies?
No. Smaller IT/ITeS units fall in Category I. Startups are covered by the policy’s ICT & deep-tech startup provisions.
Does it cover work-from-home staff?
Yes — hiring incentive and EPF reimbursement can be claimed for incremental Gujarat-based work-from-home employees.
How long is it valid?
Until 31 March 2027. Applications are handled by the Department of Science & Technology, Gujarat.
