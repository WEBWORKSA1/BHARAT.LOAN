Quick eligibility read

New or existing Micro/Small Enterprise in manufacturing or services, with Udyam registration, borrowing from an empanelled lender.

What you can get
Collateral-free credit; guarantee cover on loans up to the prevailing scheme ceiling (historically ₹2 Cr, since raised — confirm current cap with your lender).
Guarantee cover typically ranges ~75%–85% of the credit facility depending on loan size and borrower category; cover and ceilings are revised periodically by the Trust.

Who it's for

New and existing Micro & Small Enterprises in manufacturing or services that lack collateral to pledge for a bank loan.

Eligibility

  • Classified as a Micro or Small Enterprise under the MSMED Act (plant & machinery / equipment investment and annual turnover)
  • Business in manufacturing or services — trading-only units generally excluded (check if you straddle both)
  • Valid Udyam Registration
  • Clean repayment history; good credit score preferred
  • Loan from a lender empanelled as a Member Lending Institution (MLI) with CGTMSE
  • Loan must not already carry another government guarantee

How to apply

  1. Prepare your business plan and project report
  2. Approach a bank or NBFC empanelled as a CGTMSE Member Lending Institution
  3. Submit the loan application; the lender assesses eligibility
  4. On sanction, lender obtains CGTMSE guarantee cover and disburses the collateral-free loan

Frequently asked

Is CGTMSE a loan or a guarantee?
CGTMSE itself doesn't lend — it guarantees the lender, so banks extend you a collateral-free loan with reduced risk.
What does the guarantee fee cost?
Lenders pass on an Annual Guarantee Fee (a small percentage of the loan, revised periodically). Confirm the current rate with your bank.
Are trading businesses covered?
Generally no — manufacturing and services are the core. If your business straddles both, check with your lender.
