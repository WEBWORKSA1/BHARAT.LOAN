Aatmanirbhar Gujarat (MSME)

Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for Assistance to MSMEs · Est. GR dated 05/10/2022 · Industries & Mines Dept, Gujarat

Gujarat's flagship MSME package — interest subsidy up to 7%, plus capital subsidy and net SGST reimbursement, all scaled by taluka category.

Quick eligibility read

Micro/Small/Medium manufacturing or eligible-service unit in Gujarat (P&M investment ≤ ₹50 Cr), taking a term loan for new fixed assets. Exact tier set by taluka category.

What you can get
Interest subsidy of 5%–7% on the term loan (Cat 1: 7%, max ₹35L/yr, 7 yrs; Cat 2: 6%, max ₹30L/yr, 6 yrs; Cat 3 & Municipal Corp: 5%, max ₹25L/yr, 5 yrs), plus +1% for women / differently-abled / registered startups and a further +1% for entrepreneurs under 35. Micro manufacturing units also get a capital subsidy of 25%/20%/10% of the term loan (up to ₹35L/₹30L/₹10L by taluka). Manufacturing MSMEs additionally get net SGST reimbursement of 80%–100% for 10 years (up to 5%–7.5% of eligible FCI p.a.). The enterprise bears a minimum 2% interest itself.
All benefits scale by the taluka category where the unit is located (Cat 1 > Cat 2 > Cat 3 / Municipal Corporation areas). Figures are from Government Resolution No. MIS-102022-1271(1)-I(CH) dated 05/10/2022, the Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme for MSMEs, operative 05/10/2022 to 04/10/2027.

Who it's for

Micro, small and medium enterprises setting up or expanding eligible manufacturing or service units in Gujarat (fixed capital investment in plant & machinery up to ₹50 crore), financing new fixed assets through institutional credit.

Eligibility

  • MSME by P&M investment: Micro ≤ ₹1 Cr, Small ₹1–10 Cr, Medium ₹10–50 Cr
  • Eligible unit located in Gujarat with Udyam / Udyog Aadhaar or DPIIT registration
  • Investment in new fixed assets (building, new P&M, utilities) financed by a term loan — land, working capital and second-hand indigenous P&M excluded
  • Apply within 1 year of first loan disbursement, commencement of production, or GR date (whichever is later)
  • Service-sector units must be on the GR's eligible list, employ 10+ persons and hold GST registration
  • Benefit tier determined by enterprise size and taluka category

How to apply

  1. Confirm your unit, investment and taluka category under the Aatmanirbhar Gujarat MSME GR (05/10/2022)
  2. Arrange the term loan for eligible new fixed assets
  3. Apply through the Gujarat industries portal / District Industries Centre within the 1-year window
  4. Obtain the Eligibility Certificate; claim interest subsidy, capital subsidy and net SGST reimbursement per sanctioned terms

Frequently asked

Does the benefit depend on where in Gujarat I set up?
Yes — every component (interest subsidy, capital subsidy, net SGST) is tiered by taluka category, so location materially changes the subsidy. Category 1 talukas get the highest benefits. This is exactly where local advice pays off.
Can I stack this with a central subsidy?
Partly — if you also claim a central interest subsidy, the combined benefit can't reduce your own interest burden below 2%. Capital subsidy from state and centre together can't exceed the loan disbursed.
Is the capital subsidy for all MSMEs?
No — the capital subsidy component is only for micro manufacturing units. Interest subsidy and net SGST cover micro, small and medium.
