PMEGP

Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme · Est. 2008 · Ministry of MSME via KVIC / KVIB / DICs

A margin-money subsidy that funds part of a brand-new micro-enterprise — money you don't repay if the unit runs through its lock-in.

Quick eligibility read

First-time entrepreneur, 18+, starting a NEW manufacturing or service unit. Existing or already-subsidised units don't qualify.

What you can get
Margin-money subsidy of 15%–35% of project cost. Project cost ceiling: up to ₹25 lakh (manufacturing) / ₹10 lakh (service) — some current guidelines reference higher project ceilings; confirm latest.
Subsidy depends on category and location: general 15% (urban) / 25% (rural); special category (SC/ST/OBC/women/minority/differently-abled/ex-servicemen) 25% (urban) / 35% (rural). Credited to the loan account and adjusted against the loan.

Who it's for

First-time entrepreneurs (18+) starting a NEW manufacturing or service micro-enterprise. Existing or already-subsidised units are not eligible.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no upper age limit)
  • For projects above the threshold, at least 8th-standard pass
  • Only NEW projects — expansion of an existing business does not qualify
  • No income ceiling — open to any income background
  • Individuals, SHGs, registered societies, cooperative societies and charitable trusts may apply
  • Udyam registration required before physical verification / margin-money adjustment

How to apply

  1. Prepare a strong, realistic Detailed Project Report (the #1 factor at sanction)
  2. Apply online on the official PMEGP portal
  3. Implementing agency (KVIC/KVIB/DIC) reviews and forwards to the bank
  4. Interview / appraisal, then bank sanction and disbursement; subsidy adjusted into loan account
  5. Track status via applicant login on the PMEGP portal

Documents you'll need

Aadhaar card and PAN cardPassport-size photographCaste / special-category certificate (for enhanced subsidy, if applicable)Proof of educational qualificationDetailed Project Report (DPR)Machinery quotations and bank account detailsEDP training certificate, if undergone

Frequently asked

Can an existing business apply?
No — PMEGP is strictly for new units. Expansion of an existing business is handled under a separate second-loan window, not the core scheme.
Do I have to repay the subsidy?
The margin-money subsidy is adjusted into your loan and is retained if the unit runs through its lock-in period. Confirm the current lock-in with the implementing agency.
Should I pay an agent who guarantees approval?
No. The official portal explicitly warns against middlemen. Approval depends on a bankable project report, not a fixer.
