Quick eligibility read

DPIIT-recognised startup, incorporated within the eligibility window, with an innovative scalable idea — applying through an approved incubator.

What you can get
Up to ₹20 lakh as a grant (non-dilutive) for proof-of-concept, prototype or product trials; up to ₹50 lakh as convertible debentures or debt for market entry, commercialisation or scaling.
SISFS is disbursed through DPIIT-approved incubators, not directly. The grant portion does not need repayment; the convertible portion must be repaid or converts to equity per terms.

Who it's for

DPIIT-recognised startups in early stages needing seed capital to validate, prototype, or take a product to market without immediately raising equity.

Eligibility

  • DPIIT-recognised startup (recognition is a prerequisite)
  • Incorporated within the scheme's age window at application
  • An innovative product/service with a scalable business model
  • Not received more than a capped amount of prior monetary support under other central/state schemes (per scheme terms)
  • Application made to a DPIIT-approved incubator

How to apply

  1. Get DPIIT recognition first (prerequisite)
  2. Apply on the Startup India Seed Fund portal, selecting DPIIT-approved incubators
  3. The incubator evaluates and, if selected, disburses in milestones
  4. Use grant for validation/prototype; convertible/debt for scaling per the agreement

Documents you'll need

DPIIT recognition certificatePitch deck / business planIncorporation documentsFounder details and the innovation narrativeFinancials / fund-utilisation plan

Frequently asked

Do I repay the seed fund?
The grant (up to ₹20L) is non-dilutive and not repaid. The convertible portion (up to ₹50L) is repaid or converts to equity per terms.
Do I need DPIIT recognition first?
Yes — SISFS is only open to DPIIT-recognised startups, so recognition is step zero.
