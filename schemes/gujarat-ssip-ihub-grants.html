SSIP 2.0 & i-Hub grants: ₹2.5 lakh to ₹10 lakh for student founders

Student Startup & Innovation Policy 2.0 · Education Department · i-Hub Gujarat (Startup Srujan Seed Support)

Gujarat funds student and young innovators step by step — up to ₹2.5 lakh for a prototype, then ₹2.5–10 lakh in seed support, plus testing and pilot grants of up to ₹10 lakh each.

Quick eligibility read

Student, recent alumnus or dropout of a Gujarat school, college or university with an idea or prototype — up to 35 years for S4 seed support.

What you can get
A prototype grant of up to ₹2.5 lakh, seed support of ₹2.5–10 lakh, and testing and pilot grants of up to ₹10 lakh each — all non-repayable.
Grants are released in tranches against milestones. SSIP beneficiaries remain eligible for the state startup scheme later.

SSIP 2.0 runs from 11 January 2022 to 31 March 2027 under Gujarat’s Education Department; i-Hub (Gujarat Student Startup & Innovation Hub) is its nodal hub. IP filing support is also available.

Who it’s for

Students and young founders in Gujarat — from Class 9 students with an innovation to postgraduates and alumni building deep-tech, hardware, agri-tech, health-tech and social-impact startups.

Eligibility

  • Student, alumnus or dropout of a Gujarat educational institution
  • Age up to 35 for Startup Srujan Seed Support
  • Original innovation at idea, prototype or early-product stage
  • Application through the institution (SSIP) or i-Hub (S4)
  • Milestone-based use of funds

How to apply

  1. Register on ssipgujarat.in through your institution’s SSIP cell
  2. Pitch your proof of concept to the institutional committee
  3. For seed money, apply to i-Hub’s Startup Srujan Seed Support cohort
  4. Present to the screening committee
  5. Receive funds in tranches and report milestones

Frequently asked

What is SSIP 2.0?
Gujarat’s Student Startup and Innovation Policy 2.0 (2022–27). It funds student ideas through their schools, colleges and universities — up to ₹2.5 lakh per proof of concept or prototype for higher-education students and ₹20,000 for school students.
What is Startup Srujan Seed Support (S4)?
An i-Hub Gujarat grant of ₹2.5 to ₹10 lakh for innovators up to 35 — students, alumni or dropouts from Gujarat institutions — to take a prototype to the product and market stage.
Do I need DPIIT recognition?
Not for SSIP or S4. For the Gujarat startup scheme and Startup India Seed Fund, recognition and an incubator are generally needed.
How do I apply?
Register on ssipgujarat.in through your institution for SSIP; apply to i-Hub Gujarat (ihubgujarat.in) for S4, testing and pilot grants. Windows open in cohorts.
