Maharashtra Industries, Investment & Services Policy 2025

Maharashtra Industries, Investment & Services Policy 2025 · Issued 31 December 2025 · Industries Department, Maharashtra

Maharashtra’s new umbrella incentive policy — SGST-linked investment subsidy worth 30% to 100% of fixed capital investment depending on location, plus capital subsidy for thrust-sector MSMEs and a first-of-its-kind package for services.

Overall ceiling
30%–100% of FCI
MSME capital subsidy
20% FCI, cap ₹25 Cr
Period
5 years from Dec 2025
Zones
A · B · C · D · D+

Quick eligibility read

New or expanding manufacturing or eligible service enterprise in Maharashtra (MSME to mega), investing in eligible fixed capital and meeting local-employment conditions. Benefits rise steeply in less-developed zones.

What you can get
An investment promotion subsidy linked to SGST — 100% of gross SGST on in-state first sales — within an overall ceiling of 30% to 100% of fixed capital investment depending on zone, plus a 20% capital subsidy (up to ₹25 crore) for thrust-sector MSMEs, stamp-duty and electricity-duty exemptions, EPF reimbursement and technology support.
Policy period: five years from issue on 31 December 2025, or until replaced. Figures here are compiled from professional summaries of the policy (PwC, ICAI compilation); some components depend on operational guidelines and sector policies — always confirm the exact GR for your sector and taluka.

Key incentive components

  • Investment promotion subsidy: 100% of gross SGST on first sale within Maharashtra, within the zone ceiling and time limit
  • Capital subsidy (thrust-sector MSMEs): 20% of eligible FCI, capped at ₹25 crore (annual cap ₹5 crore)
  • Interest subsidy: reported at up to 5% p.a., capped at ₹1 crore — confirm in the operational GR
  • Stamp duty: 50–100% exemption by zone; electricity duty exemption for eligible MSMEs in Groups C, D, D+
  • Power tariff subsidy: ₹1 per unit for 3 years for eligible new units in Groups D, D+ and below (cap ₹1 crore)
  • EPF reimbursement: 50% of employer contribution for 5 years in eligible zones
  • Technology upgradation: 50% of additional equipment, up to ₹25 lakh; energy-efficiency support up to ₹5 lakh
  • PLI for export units: 1–1.5% of incremental turnover for 5 years

MSME thresholds follow the national definition. 80% of direct employees must be local, and investment must be completed within the prescribed timelines.

Who it’s for

Manufacturers and service businesses of every size — micro to ultra-mega — investing in Maharashtra, especially in Vidarbha, Marathwada and other less-industrialised districts where ceilings are highest.

Eligibility

  • Enterprise in an eligible sector (manufacturing or notified services)
  • New unit or eligible expansion/diversification with fixed capital investment
  • Located in Maharashtra — benefits depend on taluka group (A to D+) and special-region status
  • 80% of direct employees from Maharashtra (local employment condition)
  • Investment completed within prescribed timelines; GST registration
  • Services: minimum employment thresholds by area category

How to apply

  1. Map your taluka group and sector to the policy’s ceilings and thrust-sector lists
  2. Register the project through MAITRI / the Directorate of Industries
  3. Apply for the eligibility certificate within the prescribed time after starting commercial production
  4. Claim the SGST-linked subsidy and other components annually against the eligibility certificate

Documents you’ll need

Frequently asked

What is the Maharashtra IIS Policy 2025?
The Maharashtra Industries, Investment and Services Policy 2025 is the state’s new umbrella incentive policy, issued on 31 December 2025 for five years. It replaces the earlier Package Scheme of Incentives with zone-wise incentives for manufacturing and, for the first time on this scale, the services sector.
How are incentives decided?
Mainly by location: talukas are grouped A, B, C, D and D+, with higher ceilings for Vidarbha, Marathwada and other less-developed regions. The overall incentive ceiling for manufacturing ranges from 30% of fixed capital investment in Group A to 100% in no-industry, Naxalism-affected and aspirational districts.
What do MSMEs get?
The main benefit is an SGST-linked investment promotion subsidy within the zone ceiling. Thrust-sector MSMEs (such as export or circular-economy units) can get a capital subsidy of 20% of eligible fixed capital investment, capped at ₹25 crore. Other support includes electricity-duty and stamp-duty exemptions in eligible zones, EPF reimbursement, technology-upgradation and energy-efficiency support.
Is there anything for women or SC/ST entrepreneurs?
Yes — for example, electricity-duty exemption is extended to Group A and B units fully owned by women, SC/ST or persons with disabilities that meet the workforce condition. Sector policies such as the textile policy add extra capital subsidy for these groups.
Where do I apply?
Through the state’s investor facilitation system (MAITRI) and the Directorate of Industries / District Industries Centre, as notified in the policy’s operational guidelines.
Where an advisor helps

Thrust-sector lists, local-employment rules and certificate deadlines decide whether the money actually flows.
