NSIC at a glance

If you're a Udyam-registered micro or small enterprise, NSIC is the single PSU that consolidates a suite of central supports — tender access, raw-material credit, credit facilitation and marketing. None of it is direct cash subsidy, but each programme reduces a specific operating cost or unlocks a specific revenue channel.

The NSIC portfolio of schemes

1. Single Point Registration Scheme (SPRS)Most claimed

Registers your MSE with NSIC for government tender participation. Concrete benefits: exemption from earnest money deposit (EMD), free issue of tender documents, and access to 358 items reserved for exclusive purchase from the small-scale sector. Plus eligibility for the 25% mandatory MSME procurement share under the Public Procurement Policy.

2. Raw Material Assistance Scheme

NSIC procures bulk raw materials (steel, aluminium, paraffin wax, etc.) and supplies to MSEs on 90-day credit. NSIC bears the credit risk; MSE gets bulk pricing without locking up working capital.

3. Bank Credit Facilitation Scheme

NSIC partners with public and private sector banks to facilitate term and working-capital loans for MSEs. Acts as a sourcing/sponsorship layer that improves MSE access to bank credit. No direct lending — NSIC is a facilitator.

4. Performance Bank Guarantee (Bid Bond) Scheme

For MSEs participating in government tenders, NSIC issues performance / bid-bond guarantees against a margin deposit — saving the working-capital lock-up that a bank bond would require.

5. Marketing Support — Exhibitions, B2B Portal

NSIC organises domestic exhibitions, international trade fair participation (with partial subsidy of stall and travel costs), buyer-seller meets and a B2B portal. Marketing reach for units that can't fund participation alone.

Eligibility (across NSIC schemes)

  • Udyam-registered micro or small enterprise (MSE only — medium and large are excluded from most NSIC programmes)
  • Active manufacturing or service operations (some programmes require commercial production for at least one year)
  • Not a defaulter to any bank or financial institution
  • For SPRS specifically: also subject to commercial / technical inspection by NSIC's empanelled inspecting agency
  • For Raw Material Assistance: bank guarantee or other collateral against the 90-day credit

How to apply

  1. Confirm Udyam registration is active — NSIC schemes require this
  2. Identify which NSIC sub-scheme matches your need (SPRS for tender access, raw material for input credit, marketing for trade fair access)
  3. Apply through the NSIC portal (nsic.co.in) or through a regional NSIC office
  4. For SPRS: undergo technical inspection by NSIC's empanelled inspecting agency
  5. NSIC issues registration / sanctions credit / books exhibition space depending on scheme

Frequently asked

What is NSIC?
The National Small Industries Corporation is a central PSU under the Ministry of MSME that runs a portfolio of support schemes for micro and small enterprises — tender registration, raw material credit, credit facilitation, performance guarantee, and marketing support.
What is Single Point Registration (SPRS)?
SPRS gives micro and small enterprises three concrete benefits in government procurement: exemption from EMD, free tender documents, and access to 358 items reserved for exclusive purchase from the small-scale sector.
How does NSIC raw material assistance work?
NSIC procures bulk raw materials and supplies to MSEs on 90-day credit, with NSIC bearing the credit risk. Saves working-capital tie-up and gives bulk-rate access for small units.
Are NSIC schemes free?
SPRS has a one-time registration fee and renewal cost. Raw material assistance and bank guarantee require collateral / margin deposit. Marketing programmes typically share costs with participants. Bank credit facilitation has no direct fee but the underlying loan has bank charges.
Can I use NSIC alongside other MSME schemes?
Yes — NSIC is administrative and procurement-focused, not a competing subsidy. CGTMSE, Mudra, PMEGP, MSME 59-Min Loan all stack with NSIC registration. SPRS especially complements any growth-stage MSME credit scheme.
