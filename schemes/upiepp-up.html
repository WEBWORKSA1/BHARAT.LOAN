UP Industrial Investment & Employment Promotion Policy 2022

UPIEPP 2022 — Capital Subsidy / Net SGST / PLI Top-Up for Large Industry · Est. 2022 · Invest UP / Industries Dept, UP

UP's umbrella large-industry policy — a one-time choice of capital subsidy, net SGST reimbursement, or PLI top-up, for Large / Mega / Super-Mega / Ultra-Mega projects.

Quick eligibility read

Large industrial / manufacturing project in UP meeting the Large-category capital threshold, willing to meet annual UP-youth employment conditions.

What you can get
A one-time, irreversible choice of ONE of three mutually-exclusive incentive options: (1) capital subsidy, (2) net SGST reimbursement, or (3) a top-up on Production-Linked Incentive (PLI) benefits. Total benefits under the policy are capped at 200% of eligible capital investment over the incentive period. Investments are categorised Large / Mega / Super-Mega / Ultra-Mega by capital size. Employment conditions apply (Large units must employ at least 300 UP youth/year; Mega at least 600/year), with a booster subsidy where 75% of employment goes to women. Stamp-duty exemption: 100% in Purvanchal & Bundelkhand, 75% in Madhyanchal & Paschimanchal (except Gautam Budh Nagar / Ghaziabad).
Notified 2022 by the UP Industries Department / Invest UP. The three options are mutually exclusive and the choice is one-time, so the selection should be financially modelled before committing. Sector-specific anchor incentives (e.g. bioplastics anchor units ₹1,000 Cr+: 5% interest subsidy 7 yrs, 100% SGST 10 yrs, 100% electricity-duty exemption 10 yrs) sit alongside this policy.

Who it's for

Large, Mega, Super-Mega and Ultra-Mega manufacturing and industrial projects investing in Uttar Pradesh, including expansion/diversification of existing units above the threshold.

Eligibility

  • Project categorised Large / Mega / Super-Mega / Ultra-Mega by eligible capital investment
  • Located in Uttar Pradesh
  • Meets the annual UP-youth employment condition for its category (e.g. Large = 300/yr, Mega = 600/yr)
  • Eligible fixed capital investment as defined in the policy
  • Selects exactly one of the three incentive options (capital subsidy / net SGST / PLI top-up)

How to apply

  1. Categorise the project (Large → Ultra-Mega) and model which of the three options pays most
  2. Register and apply via Invest UP / Nivesh Mitra single-window portal
  3. Submit DPR, approvals and the UP-youth employment plan
  4. Obtain the eligibility / incentive sanction; claim the selected option against milestones over the incentive period

Documents you'll need

Detailed project report with capital-investment planLand, environmental and statutory approvalsEmployment / hiring plan for UP youthGST registrationApplication through Invest UP / the Nivesh Mitra single-window portal

Frequently asked

Can I take all three incentives?
No — the policy forces a one-time choice of exactly one: capital subsidy, net SGST reimbursement, or PLI top-up. Choosing wrong is expensive, which is why it should be modelled first.
Is there an overall cap?
Yes — total benefits cannot exceed 200% of your eligible capital investment over the incentive period.
What are the employment conditions?
By category — a Large unit must employ at least 300 UP youth per year, a Mega unit at least 600, with a booster subsidy if 75% of employment is women.
Where an advisor helps

UPIEPP's one-time choice between capital subsidy, net SGST and PLI top-up is the single highest-stakes decision in the policy — the right option can differ by crores depending on your turnover, margin and capex profile, and it can't be reversed. We financially model all three against your projections before you commit, and structure the employment plan to clear the category conditions.
