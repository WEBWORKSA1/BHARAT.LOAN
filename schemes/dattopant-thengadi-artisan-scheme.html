Dattopant Thengadi Artisan Interest Subsidy: ₹1 lakh loan for artisans

Dattopant Thengadi Artisan Interest Subsidy Scheme · Industries & Mines Department · District Industries Centres, Gujarat

For registered artisans in Gujarat — a bank loan of up to ₹1 lakh for tools, machinery or working capital, with a 20%–25% margin subsidy and a 7% interest subsidy for three years.

Loan
Up to ₹1 lakh
Margin subsidy
20%–25%
Interest subsidy
7% for 3 yrs
Needs
Artisan ID card

Quick eligibility read

Registered artisan in Gujarat with an Artisan Identity Card who needs a small loan for tools, equipment or working capital.

What you can get
A loan of up to ₹1 lakh with a 20% margin subsidy (25% for reserved categories, women and persons with disabilities) and 7% interest subsidy for three years.
Designed for craftspeople who need small-ticket working capital rather than a full project loan.

Who it’s for

Craftspeople and artisans — weavers, potters, carpenters, leather workers, embroiderers, metal workers and other traditional trades — registered with Gujarat’s cottage-industries system.

Eligibility

  • Registered artisan holding an Artisan Identity Card
  • Resident of Gujarat
  • No education or income restriction
  • Loan for machinery, tools or working capital for the craft

How to apply

  1. Get your Artisan Identity Card if you don’t have one
  2. Collect the application form from your District Industries Centre
  3. Attach quotations and documents and submit at the DIC
  4. DIC recommends the case to the bank, which sanctions the loan
  5. Margin and interest subsidy are released as per the scheme

Documents you’ll need

Artisan Identity CardAadhaar cardRation cardBusiness address proofQuotationsElectricity billPassport-size photos

Frequently asked

Who can use the Dattopant Thengadi scheme?
Artisans registered in Gujarat who hold an Artisan Identity Card. There is no education or income restriction.
How much loan can I get?
Up to ₹1 lakh from a bank, for tools, machinery or working capital.
What subsidy is available?
A margin subsidy of 20% for general male applicants and 25% for reserved-category applicants, women and persons with disabilities, plus a 7% interest subsidy for up to three years.
Where do I apply?
At your District Industries Centre, with your Artisan Identity Card, Aadhaar, quotations and the other listed documents.
