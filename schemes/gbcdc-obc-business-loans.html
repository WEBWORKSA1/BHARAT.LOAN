GBCDC business loans for OBC / SEBC entrepreneurs in Gujarat

Gujarat Backward Class Development Corporation (with NBCFDC) · Social Justice & Empowerment Department

Low-interest business loans for backward-class (SEBC/OBC) entrepreneurs in Gujarat — ₹1.25 lakh micro-finance, ₹2 lakh for women under New Swarnima, and Swayam Saksham for professionals — at about 5%.

Micro finance
Up to ₹1.25 lakh
Women (Swarnima)
Up to ₹2 lakh
Interest
5% a year
Income limit
₹3 lakh/yr

Quick eligibility read

SEBC/OBC resident of Gujarat aged 21–45, family income up to ₹3 lakh, with skills or experience for the business.

What you can get
Loans of up to ₹1.25 lakh (₹2 lakh for women) at 5% a year, plus Swayam Saksham loans for professionals setting up practice.
Repayment over 48–60 monthly instalments.

GBCDC loan options

SchemeLoanInterest & repayment

New Swarnima (women)Up to ₹2 lakh5% a year · 60 monthly instalments
Swayam Saksham (professionals)Per project — for doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs and other professionals setting up practiceLow interest — see portal

Common criteria: socially and educationally backward class (SEBC/OBC), family income up to ₹3 lakh, age 21–45, relevant skill or experience.

Who it’s for

Backward-class youth and women starting small trades, services and crafts, and qualified professionals opening clinics, offices and practices.

Eligibility

  • Member of a socially and educationally backward class (SEBC/OBC) in Gujarat
  • Aged 21–45
  • Family income up to ₹3 lakh a year
  • Experience or skills for the business
  • No outstanding loan from another government corporation

How to apply

  1. Register on gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in with Aadhaar and OTP
  2. Choose the scheme and fill the application
  3. Upload caste, income, identity and bank documents
  4. Submit and track with your application ID
  5. Loan is sanctioned and disbursed after verification

Documents you’ll need

Caste certificateIncome certificateAadhaar cardAge proofBank passbookResidence proofBusiness quotationsEducation / professional certificates (Swayam Saksham)

Frequently asked

What loans does GBCDC give?
Gujarat Backward Class Development Corporation, with the National Backward Classes Finance & Development Corporation (NBCFDC), gives micro-finance loans up to ₹1.25 lakh, New Swarnima loans up to ₹2 lakh for women, and Swayam Saksham loans for professionals — at about 5% interest.
Who is eligible?
Members of socially and educationally backward classes (SEBC/OBC) in Gujarat aged 21–45, with family income up to ₹3 lakh a year and relevant skills or experience.
How do I apply?
Online on gbcdconline.gujarat.gov.in — register, choose the scheme, upload caste, income and identity documents, and submit.
Can I get a bigger loan?
For larger projects, combine with SVBY (subsidy on loans up to ₹25 lakh), PMEGP or Mudra.
