Quick eligibility read

Textile unit in Gujarat in a covered activity (garments, technical textiles, weaving, knitting, dyeing/processing, MMF spinning from PSF/VSF), investing in eligible plant/machinery with institutional finance.

What you can get
Capital subsidy of 10%–35% of eligible fixed capital investment (eFCI), scaled by taluka category and activity, up to a maximum of around ₹100 crore. Credit-linked interest subsidy of 5%–7% of eFCI for 5–8 years, with reimbursement capped at 2%–3% of eFCI per annum by taluka. A power-tariff subsidy of ₹1 per unit, plus payroll assistance of ₹3,000–₹5,000/month per female worker and ₹2,000–₹3,000 per male worker for up to 5 years for labour-intensive units.
Figures are from the Gujarat Textile Policy 2024 (announced October 2024 by the Industries & Mines Department). The capital subsidy band and interest-subsidy cap vary by taluka category and by which activity group your unit falls in; confirm your segment's exact entitlement.

Who it's for

Textile manufacturers in Gujarat across the covered value chain — garments/apparel/made-ups and technical textiles (Activity 1); weaving, knitting, dyeing & processing, texturizing, twisting, embroidery and MMF spinning from PSF/VSF (Activity 2). Cotton and synthetic filament yarn spinning are excluded.

Eligibility

  • Textile-sector unit in Gujarat in a covered activity group
  • Investment in eligible plant & machinery within a covered segment (cotton / synthetic filament yarn spinning excluded)
  • Institutional term-loan financing for the project
  • New unit or existing unit undertaking expansion / diversification
  • Compliance with the 2024 policy's registration, employment and segment criteria

How to apply

  1. Confirm your activity group and machinery are covered under the Textile Policy 2024
  2. Arrange eligible term-loan financing
  3. Apply through the Commissioner of Textiles / Gujarat industries portal
  4. Claim capital subsidy, interest subsidy, power-tariff and payroll assistance per sanctioned terms

Documents you'll need

Udyam / unit registrationGujarat unit and land documentsTerm loan sanction and machinery invoicesSegment-specific project reportEmployment records (for payroll assistance / labour-intensive benefits)

Frequently asked

Which textile segments are covered?
Two activity groups: (1) garments, apparel, made-ups and technical textiles; (2) weaving, knitting, dyeing & processing, texturizing, twisting, embroidery and MMF spinning from PSF/VSF. Cotton and synthetic filament yarn spinning are specifically excluded.
How much of my machine cost comes back?
Industry estimates put total recovery at roughly 25%–40% of machine cost once capital subsidy, interest subsidy and power benefits are combined — but it depends on taluka and activity. We model it for your specific case.
Is there help for labour-heavy units?
Yes — payroll assistance per worker (higher for women) and a separate fiscal-incentive track for labour-intensive units with very large headcounts.
