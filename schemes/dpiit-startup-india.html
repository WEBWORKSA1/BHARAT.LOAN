DPIIT Recognition + 80-IAC

Startup India — DPIIT Recognition & Section 80-IAC Tax Exemption · Est. 2016 · DPIIT / Inter-Ministerial Board

Quick eligibility read

Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership or Cooperative — incorporated from April 2016, within the age/turnover caps, with a genuine innovation angle. Not sole proprietorships.

What you can get
DPIIT recognition: free, approved in ~1–14 days. Unlocks Section 80-IAC (100% income-tax exemption for 3 consecutive years out of the first 10), angel-tax relief, 80% patent fee rebate, 50% trademark rebate, self-certification under labour & environment laws, and Seed Fund / GeM access.
Critical distinction: DPIIT recognition is necessary but NOT sufficient for the 80-IAC tax holiday. 80-IAC is a separate Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB) application, reviewed within ~120 days, historically approved for only a small fraction of recognised startups — the innovation narrative is the #1 rejection reason.

Who it's for

Private Limited companies, LLPs, Partnership firms and (2026) Cooperative Societies — incorporated from April 2016, under the age and turnover caps, with a genuine innovation / scalability angle.

Eligibility

  • Entity is Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership Firm or Cooperative Society (not a sole proprietorship)
  • Incorporated on or after 1 April 2016
  • Within the recognition age window and below the turnover cap per the current framework
  • Working on innovation/improvement of products/services, or a scalable, employment-generating model
  • For 80-IAC specifically: only Pvt Ltd and LLPs are eligible for the tax holiday

How to apply

  1. Incorporate your entity (Pvt Ltd / LLP / Partnership / Cooperative)
  2. Apply for DPIIT recognition online via NSWS or the Startup India portal — free, no government fee
  3. Recognition certificate typically issued within days
  4. Separately apply for Section 80-IAC to the Inter-Ministerial Board with financials and innovation narrative
  5. IMB reviews (~120 days); if approved, claim the tax holiday in a profitable year within the first 10

Documents you'll need

Certificate of incorporationDetails of directors / partnersA clear innovation note — problem, solution, technology, evidence, scalabilityWebsite / pitch deck / product details (recommended)For 80-IAC: DPIIT certificate, financial statements, strong innovation narrative for the IMB

Frequently asked

Is DPIIT recognition the same as the tax holiday?
No — and this trips up most founders. Recognition is easy and fast; the 80-IAC tax holiday is a separate, IMB-reviewed application with a low historical approval rate.
Does it cost anything?
DPIIT recognition is genuinely free — zero government fee. Anyone charging a 'government fee' is not authorised.
Can a sole proprietor apply?
No. You need a Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership or Cooperative. Incorporate first.
