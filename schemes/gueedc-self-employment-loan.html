GUEEDC Nana Vyavasay loan: ₹10 lakh at 5% for unreserved-category founders

Self-Employment Scheme (Nana Vyavasay Mate Loan Yojana) · Gujarat Unreserved Educational & Economic Development Corporation

A ₹10 lakh business loan at 5% simple interest (4% for women) from Gujarat’s Unreserved Development Corporation — for general-category families earning up to ₹6 lakh a year.

Loan
Up to ₹10 lakh
Interest
5% (women 4%)
Repayment
5 years
Income limit
₹6 lakh/yr

Last reviewed 25 Nov 2026 · Reviewed by the BHARAT.LOAN advisory desk · Official portal ↗

At a glanceWhat you getWho it’s forEligibilityHow to applyDocumentsFAQ
Quick eligibility read

Unreserved (general) category Gujarat resident aged 18–50, family income up to ₹6 lakh a year, starting a small business.

What you can get
A loan of up to ₹10 lakh at 5% simple interest (4% for women), repayable over five years with a three-month moratorium.
Up to 5 beneficiaries per village, and at least 10% of loans go to women.

Who it’s for

Young general-category entrepreneurs from modest-income families — shops, medical and grocery stores, garment and book stores, small service businesses and self-employment vehicles.

Eligibility

  • Resident of Gujarat from the unreserved (general) category
  • Aged 18–50
  • Family income up to ₹6 lakh a year
  • Business started within three months of the loan
  • One beneficiary per family

How to apply

  1. Register on the GUEEDC online portal and choose the self-employment scheme
  2. Fill the form, upload documents and submit
  3. Send the signed printout to the Deputy Director / District Social Welfare Officer
  4. After in-principle approval, upload documents within 7 days and submit physical copies within 30 days
  5. Loan is disbursed; repay monthly over 5 years

Documents you’ll need

Aadhaar cardPAN cardNon-reserved category certificateIncome certificateLeaving certificate (age proof)Quotations / project detailsBank passbookGuarantor documents

Frequently asked

Who can take the GUEEDC self-employment loan?
Gujarat residents from the unreserved (general) category aged 18–50 with family income up to ₹6 lakh a year. One person per family.
What is the interest rate?
5% simple interest a year — 4% for women — repaid in monthly instalments over 5 years, starting three months after the business begins.
How much can I borrow?
Up to ₹10 lakh or the actual project cost, whichever is lower, for businesses such as grocery, medical, garment or book stores and self-employment vehicles.
Where do I apply?
Online on the GUEEDC portal (gueedc.gujarat.gov.in), then submit the signed printout to the Deputy Director (Non-Reserved Category) / District Social Welfare Officer.
