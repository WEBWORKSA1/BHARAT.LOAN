Gujarat SpaceTech Policy 2025-30: India’s first state space policy

Gujarat SpaceTech Policy 2025-30 · Gujarat State Electronics Mission · Department of Science & Technology

Support for satellite makers, ground-segment and space-application companies in Gujarat — launch-cost assistance up to ₹5 crore, patent support and subsidised test facilities on top of electronics or IT incentives.

Launch support
25%, max ₹5 Cr/launch
Patents
30%, max ₹50 lakh
Test facilities
25% for 5 yrs
Period
2025–2030

Quick eligibility read

Space-sector manufacturer, ground-segment or space-application / design company setting up in Gujarat.

What you can get
25% of launch fees (up to ₹5 crore per launch) for satellites made in Gujarat, 30% of Indian patent costs (up to ₹50 lakh) and 25% off common test-facility charges for 5 years.
Manufacturing units also get Electronics Policy incentives; ground-segment and application units get IT/ITeS Policy incentives.

Who it’s for

Satellite and subsystem manufacturers, ground-station and space-application companies, design houses and space startups.

Eligibility

  • Space-sector activity covered by the policy
  • Located in Gujarat
  • Satellites manufactured in Gujarat for launch support
  • Patents filed and granted within 5 years of commercial operation

How to apply

  1. Apply to the Gujarat State Electronics Mission
  2. Technical Advisory Committee review
  3. State-Level Empowered Committee approval
  4. Claim incentives after commercial operation

Documents you’ll need

Company documentsProject reportLaunch contracts (for launch support)Patent filingsFacility usage invoices

Frequently asked

Is Gujarat the first state with a SpaceTech policy?
Yes — the Gujarat SpaceTech Policy 2025-30, announced in April 2025, was India’s first state space-technology policy.
What incentives does it give?
Space manufacturing gets Electronics Policy incentives; ground-segment, applications and design units get IT/ITeS Policy incentives; plus 25% of launch fees (up to ₹5 crore per launch), 30% of patent costs (up to ₹50 lakh) and a 25% subsidy on common-facility charges for 5 years.
Can space startups benefit?
Yes, through the ICT & deep-tech startup provisions of the IT/ITeS Policy and the Gujarat startup scheme.
Who is the nodal agency?
The Gujarat State Electronics Mission, with a Technical Advisory Committee and a State-Level Empowered Committee.
