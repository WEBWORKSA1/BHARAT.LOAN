Quick eligibility read

GST-registered, IT-compliant business with 6+ months of bank statements, seeking quick in-principle approval.

What you can get
In-principle approval of term loans and working-capital loans up to ₹1 crore through the online platform, before the loan moves to the chosen bank for formal sanction.
The 59-minute output is an in-principle approval, not final disbursement — the loan still completes formal processing at the selected bank. It can be paired with CGTMSE for collateral-free cover.

Who it's for

Existing MSMEs with digital financial records (GST, income tax, bank statements) wanting fast in-principle approval to accelerate the bank loan process.

Eligibility

  • Business is GST-registered
  • Income-tax compliant (ITR available)
  • At least 6 months of bank statements available
  • Existing business with a financial track record (less suited to brand-new units)

How to apply

  1. Register on the psbloansin59minutes.com platform
  2. Upload GST, ITR and bank statement data
  3. Receive in-principle approval (up to ₹1 Cr) within ~59 minutes
  4. Select a bank; complete formal sanction and disbursement with that bank

Documents you'll need

GST identification and returnsIncome-tax returns6 months' bank statements (or bank statement upload)Director/partner KYC and ownership details

Frequently asked

Does the money arrive in 59 minutes?
No — the 59 minutes is in-principle approval. Formal sanction and disbursement still happen at the chosen bank afterwards.
Can I combine it with a collateral-free guarantee?
Yes — the underlying bank loan can be backed by CGTMSE for collateral-free cover, subject to the bank.
