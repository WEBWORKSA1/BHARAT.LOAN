Quick eligibility read

New or expanding large industrial project in Gujarat with P&M investment above ₹50 crore. Terms scale by taluka category and General vs Thrust sector.

What you can get
Net SGST reimbursement of 80%–100% of net SGST paid in cash for 10 years, capped at 5%–8% of eligible FCI per annum by taluka and sector (Cat 1 General 100% up to 7.5% eFCI; Cat 1 Thrust up to 8%; Cat 2 90% up to 6.5%; Cat 3 & Municipal Corp 80% up to 5%). Interest subsidy at 7% on the term loan, capped at 1%–1.2% of eFCI per annum for 6–10 years by sector and taluka. EPF reimbursement up to ₹1,800/employee/month for 10 years, plus electricity-duty exemption for 5 years. The unit bears a minimum 2% interest itself.
This is the Large Industries & Thrust Sector arm of the Aatmanirbhar Gujarat Scheme — Government Resolution dated 05/10/2022, operative to 04/10/2027 — for manufacturers with fixed capital investment in plant & machinery above ₹50 crore. (MSMEs below that threshold fall under the Aatmanirbhar Gujarat MSME scheme.) Mega Industries (very large investment + high employment) have a separate enhanced tier.

Who it's for

Large manufacturing projects in Gujarat with fixed capital investment in plant & machinery above ₹50 crore — new units or existing units undertaking expansion / diversification. Thrust-sector projects get enhanced terms.

Eligibility

  • Manufacturing project with P&M investment above ₹50 crore (large industry threshold)
  • Located in Gujarat; benefit scaled by taluka category and General vs Thrust sector
  • New project, or existing unit undertaking qualifying expansion / diversification
  • Eligible fixed capital investment as defined in the GR (new building, new P&M, utilities)
  • Compliance with employment, GST-registration and other policy conditions

How to apply

  1. Confirm the project crosses the ₹50 Cr P&M threshold and its taluka / sector classification
  2. Compile the project, investment and approvals documentation
  3. Apply to the Industries & Mines Department / Gujarat industries portal
  4. Obtain Provisional then Final Eligibility Certificate; claim net SGST reimbursement, interest subsidy, EPF and ED benefits per sanctioned terms

Frequently asked

How much does taluka location matter?
Significantly — net SGST (80%–100%) and the per-annum eFCI caps (5%–8%) both scale by taluka category, and Thrust-sector status raises them further. Over a 10-year window this is worth crores. Site selection is a strategic incentive decision.
Is this a separate scheme from the MSME one?
Same umbrella policy (Aatmanirbhar Gujarat 2022, GR 05/10/2022), split by size: above ₹50 Cr P&M = large/thrust arm; at or below = MSME arm. The two are filed differently.
What counts as a Thrust Sector?
The GR lists specific priority manufacturing sectors that receive higher SGST caps and shorter qualifying periods. We confirm whether your product qualifies.
