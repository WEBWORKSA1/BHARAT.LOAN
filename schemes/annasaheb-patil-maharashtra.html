Annasaheb Patil Loan Scheme: interest reimbursement on business loans (Maharashtra)

Annasaheb Patil Arthik Magas Vikas Mahamandal · Interest Reimbursement schemes IR-I & IR-II

Maharashtra’s interest-reimbursement scheme for eligible entrepreneurs — take a business loan from a bank and get your interest (up to 12% p.a.) paid back, on loans up to ₹15 lakh for individuals and up to ₹50 lakh for groups.

Interest refunded
Up to 12% p.a.
IR-I loan
Up to ₹15 lakh
IR-II loan
₹25–50 lakh (group)

₹4.5L / ₹15L

Quick eligibility read

Maharashtra resident, mainly from the Maratha community or communities without a separate corporation, family income within the non-creamy layer (up to ₹8 lakh for IR-I), taking a business loan from an eligible bank.

What you can get
Interest reimbursement up to 12% per annum on business loans: IR-I (individual) on loans up to ₹15 lakh with total reimbursement up to ₹4.5 lakh; IR-II (groups) on loans of ₹25 lakh (2 members) to ₹50 lakh (5+ members) with reimbursement up to ₹15 lakh.
Reimbursed by the Annasaheb Patil Arthik Magas Vikas Mahamandal into your Aadhaar-linked account after each on-time EMI, for up to 5 years (up to 7 years for the enhanced IR-I limit — check your LOI). 4% of funds are reserved for divyang applicants.

Who it’s for

Individual entrepreneurs (IR-I) and groups — SHGs, partnership firms, cooperatives, companies, LLPs and eligible FPOs (IR-II) — starting or running businesses in Maharashtra with a bank loan.

Eligibility

  • Resident of Maharashtra; business located in Maharashtra
  • IR-I: men up to 45 years, women up to 55 years; IR-II members 18–45
  • Family income within the non-creamy-layer limit (up to ₹8 lakh for IR-I)
  • Loan from a CBS-enabled, RBI-regulated bank operating in Maharashtra; applicant must be the first borrower
  • IR-I: total business loan not more than ₹25 lakh
  • EMIs must be paid on time — late EMIs are not reimbursed

How to apply

  1. Register on udyog.mahaswayam.gov.in with Aadhaar OTP
  2. Choose IR-I or IR-II, fill details, accept the online affidavit, upload documents and a one-page project report
  3. Receive the Letter of Intent (LOI) online if eligible — usually within about a week
  4. Take the LOI and project report to an eligible bank and get the loan sanctioned
  5. Upload sanction letter, disbursement proof and EMI schedule; upload business photos within 6 months
  6. Pay each EMI on time, upload the bank-stamped statement and claim the interest

Documents you’ll need

Aadhaar (linked to mobile and bank account)Income certificate / non-creamy-layer proofCaste certificate as applicableDomicile proofOne-page project reportBank sanction letter, disbursement proof and EMI schedule (after loan)Business photographs (within 6 months of start)

Frequently asked

Who is the Annasaheb Patil scheme for?
Mainly Maratha-community applicants and communities in Maharashtra that do not have their own corporation, within the non-creamy-layer income limit (family income up to ₹8 lakh for IR-I). It reimburses interest on business loans taken from banks.
How much interest is reimbursed?
Interest up to 12% per annum. Under IR-I (individual) on loans up to ₹15 lakh, total reimbursement up to ₹4.5 lakh; under IR-II (group) on loans up to ₹25–50 lakh depending on members, up to ₹15 lakh.
Do I get money upfront?
No. You take a normal bank loan, pay every EMI on time, upload the repayment statement and claim — the corporation then credits the interest to your Aadhaar-linked account. Late EMIs are not reimbursed.
What is the LOI?
After you apply on udyog.mahaswayam.gov.in, the corporation issues a Letter of Intent if you are eligible. You take it to a bank to get the loan. It is valid for 6 months free, then renewable.
Is the group project loan scheme open?
The group project loan scheme (GL-I) has been reported as temporarily suspended. IR-I and IR-II are the active routes — confirm current status on the official portal.
Where an advisor helps

The scheme works only if the paperwork is flawless: LOI validity windows, first-borrower rules, Aadhaar-linked accounts and on-time EMIs with bank-stamped statements.
