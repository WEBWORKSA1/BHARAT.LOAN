Central government schemes

Credit · Tier S
CGTMSE
Collateral-free business loans, government-guaranteed so banks lend without security.
Subsidy · Tier S
PMEGP
A margin-money subsidy that funds part of a brand-new micro-enterprise — money you don't repay if the unit runs through its lock-in.
Subsidy · Tier A
CLCSS
A capital subsidy that pays back part of what you spend upgrading plant and machinery to better technology.
Credit · Tier A
PM Mudra Yojana
Collateral-free, guarantor-free micro-loans on a ladder from ₹50,000 to ₹20 lakh — built to fund the unfunded.
Subsidy · Tier S
Startup India Seed Fund
Early-stage seed capital for DPIIT-recognised startups — grants for validation, convertible/debt for scale.
Tax · Tier S
DPIIT Recognition + 80-IAC
The free, ~72-hour recognition that unlocks the entire Startup India benefit stack — and the separate, far harder tax holiday underneath it.
Credit · Tier B
MSME Loan in 59 Minutes
In-principle business loan approval in under an hour through a single online platform, before formal bank processing.
Credit · Tier B
SIDBI Direct
Direct and refinance lending from India's apex MSME development bank, for growth, machinery and working capital.
