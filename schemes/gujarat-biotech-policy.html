Gujarat Biotechnology Policy 2022-27: CAPEX, OPEX and interest support

Gujarat Biotechnology Policy 2022-27 · Gujarat State Biotechnology Mission · Department of Science & Technology

For biotech manufacturing and research units in Gujarat — up to 25% CAPEX and 15% OPEX support, a 7% interest subsidy, EPF reimbursement and electricity-duty refund.

CAPEX
Up to 25%, max ₹200 Cr
MSME cap
₹40 Cr (₹5 Cr/yr)
OPEX
15%, max ₹25 Cr/yr
Interest
7% (loans ≤ ₹100 Cr)

Last reviewed 25 Nov 2026 · Reviewed by the BHARAT.LOAN advisory desk · Official portal ↗

At a glanceWhat you getWho it’s forEligibilityHow to applyDocumentsFAQ
Quick eligibility read

Biotechnology unit — biopharma, agri/industrial biotech, vaccines, genomics, testing labs — setting up or expanding in Gujarat.

What you can get
CAPEX support of up to 25% (₹200 crore cap for large/mega/special projects; ₹40 crore for MSMEs) and OPEX support of 15% up to ₹25 crore a year.
Interest subsidy of 7% on loans up to ₹100 crore (max ₹7 crore a year), 100% electricity-duty reimbursement and EPF support (100% women / 75% men).

Who it’s for

Biotech manufacturers, contract research and testing labs, genome-sequencing and BSL-3 facilities, and biotech MSMEs in Gujarat.

Eligibility

  • Biotechnology activity covered by the policy
  • Located in Gujarat
  • New project or expansion
  • MSME (below ₹200 crore) or large/mega project
  • Registered with the Gujarat State Biotechnology Mission

How to apply

  1. Register the project with GSBTM
  2. Get in-principle approval
  3. Invest and begin operations
  4. Claim CAPEX in instalments and OPEX annually

Documents you’ll need

Company documentsProject reportCA certificatesLease / land documentsTerm-loan documentsPayroll / EPF records

Frequently asked

What does the Gujarat Biotechnology Policy 2022-27 offer?
Up to 25% CAPEX support (max ₹200 crore for large/mega/special projects; ₹40 crore for MSMEs), 15% OPEX support up to ₹25 crore a year, interest subsidy, EPF support and electricity-duty reimbursement.
How much can a biotech MSME get?
For projects below ₹200 crore, capital assistance of up to ₹40 crore in total, at up to ₹5 crore a year.
What is the interest subsidy?
7% on term loans up to ₹100 crore (max ₹7 crore a year); 3% on larger loans (max ₹20 crore a year).
Who runs it?
The Gujarat State Biotechnology Mission (GSBTM) under the Department of Science & Technology.
