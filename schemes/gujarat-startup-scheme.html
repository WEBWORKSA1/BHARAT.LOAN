Quick eligibility read

Gujarat-based seed/early-stage startup with an innovative idea, applying through an approved state nodal institution / incubator.

What you can get
Monthly sustenance allowance of ₹20,000 for one year (₹25,000 if the startup has at least one woman co-founder). Seed/product-development support up to ₹30 lakh for prototyping, raw materials, testing, market research and marketing. Up to ₹3 lakh to attend recognised national/international accelerator programs, up to ₹1 lakh for skill/managerial training, and up to ₹10 lakh extra for startups with significant social impact. A 1% additional interest subsidy (up to a 9% maximum on eligible term loans) under the MSME provisions, plus access to ₹50 lakh–₹3 crore mid-stage funding via Gujarat Venture Finance Ltd (GVFL).
Delivered through approved nodal institutions / incubators in Gujarat, with the screening committee approving each project. Amounts are from the current Gujarat startup assistance scheme (Industries Commissionerate). A startup can't simultaneously draw another state/central startup grant for the same project, though SSIP beneficiaries remain eligible.

Who it's for

Seed-stage and early-stage startups and individual innovators based in Gujarat with an innovative, scalable idea, working through an approved nodal institution.

Eligibility

  • Startup / innovator based in Gujarat with an innovative, scalable idea
  • Only seed-stage and/or early-stage startups (not formed by splitting/reconstructing an existing business)
  • Applying through an approved nodal institution / incubator, with screening-committee recommendation
  • Not simultaneously availing another Gujarat/central startup grant for the same project (SSIP beneficiaries excepted)
  • Compliance with the current Gujarat startup scheme criteria

How to apply

  1. Connect with an approved Gujarat nodal institution / incubator
  2. Submit your idea/plan for screening-committee evaluation
  3. On approval, draw the ₹20,000/month (₹25,000 with a woman co-founder) sustenance allowance for one year
  4. Claim seed support (up to ₹30L), accelerator (up to ₹3L), training (up to ₹1L) and social-impact assistance (up to ₹10L) against milestones

Frequently asked

How much is the sustenance allowance?
₹20,000 per month for one year per startup — or ₹25,000 per month if the startup has at least one woman co-founder.
Is the seed support a single grant?
No — the headline figure is cumulative across components (prototype/product development, accelerator, training, social impact). The ₹20,000/month sustenance is separate from the seed support. We map which components you can stack.
Can I combine this with central Startup India benefits?
Generally you can't draw two startup grants for the same project, but SSIP beneficiaries are exempted and central DPIIT recognition still applies. The sequencing matters — verify before assuming both.
