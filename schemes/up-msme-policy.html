Quick eligibility read

UP-registered MSME investing in quality certification, IP, ERP/ICT, environmental infrastructure, or developing an MSME park.

What you can get
Infrastructure interest subsidy up to 50% (ceiling ₹2 crore/year) for approved MSME parks / estates / flatted-factory complexes of 10 acres or more. Quality-certification (ZED, GMP, Hallmark) reimbursement up to 75% (max ₹5 lakh). Patent / GI assistance 75% (max ₹10 lakh), plus attorney-fee reimbursement of ₹50,000 (national) / ₹2,00,000 (international). ERP support 75% (max ₹1 lakh); ICT 75% (max ₹5 lakh). CETP assistance 50% (max ₹10 crore); Zero Liquid Discharge 50% (max ₹75 lakh). Stamp-duty exemption: 100% in Purvanchal & Bundelkhand, 75% in Madhyanchal & Paschimanchal (except Gautam Budh Nagar / Ghaziabad).
Notified 2022 by the UP MSME Directorate. UP has India's largest MSME base (~96 lakh units). These are reimbursement-style and infrastructure incentives that sit alongside the credit/margin-money schemes (CM-YUVA, CMYSY, ODOP) rather than replacing them.

Who it's for

Micro, small and medium enterprises in Uttar Pradesh — and MSME-park developers — investing in quality, technology, IP, environmental compliance or shared industrial infrastructure.

Eligibility

  • MSME registered and operating in Uttar Pradesh (Udyam)
  • Investment in an eligible category — quality certification, patent/GI, ERP/ICT, CETP/ZLD, or a qualifying MSME park (≥10 acres for the infra interest subsidy)
  • Compliance with the 2022 policy's per-component conditions
  • Region determines the stamp-duty exemption rate

How to apply

  1. Identify which components your investment qualifies for (quality, IP, ERP/ICT, environmental, park infra)
  2. Register the unit on the UP MSME portal (msme1connect.up.gov.in)
  3. Submit the component-specific claim with supporting invoices/certificates
  4. Reimbursement is sanctioned per the component cap once verified

Documents you'll need

Udyam RegistrationUP unit / land documentsInvoices / certificates for the claimed component (ZED, patent, ERP, etc.)Project details for park / CETP / ZLD claimsBank and financial details

Frequently asked

Is this a loan or a subsidy?
Neither in the usual sense — it's mostly reimbursement of money you've already spent on quality, IP, technology or environmental infrastructure, plus stamp-duty exemption. It complements the credit schemes.
What's the biggest-ticket item?
For park developers, the infrastructure interest subsidy (up to 50%, ₹2 Cr/year) and CETP support (50%, up to ₹10 Cr) are the largest. For a single MSME, quality, patent and ICT reimbursements add up.
