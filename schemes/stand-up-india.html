Stand-Up India: ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore loan for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs

Stand-Up India · Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance · Operated via SIDBI portal + scheduled commercial bank branches

Stand-Up India is the central government's targeted credit scheme to put SC, ST and women entrepreneurs into business ownership — a ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore composite loan from any scheduled commercial bank branch for greenfield enterprises in manufacturing, services, trading or allied agriculture.

Stand-Up India at a glance

If you're a Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe or woman entrepreneur, 18+, starting a brand-new business in manufacturing, services, trading or allied agriculture, you can apply for a composite loan of ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore from any scheduled commercial bank branch — covering up to 85% of project cost.

What you can get
A composite loan of ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore (term loan + working capital combined). Bank lends up to 85% of project cost; the entrepreneur brings 15% margin. Interest rate is bank's MCLR + tenor premium + up to 3% (regulated cap). Tenure up to 7 years including 18-month moratorium.
The Credit Guarantee Fund for Stand-Up India (CGFSI) provides collateral guarantee, similar in spirit to CGTMSE. Stand-Up India is delivered by every scheduled commercial bank branch in India — and at least one such loan per branch is a stated programme target.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above
  • Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or Woman entrepreneur
  • Greenfield enterprise only (first-time entrepreneur in the activity — not for buying out or expanding an existing unit)
  • Activity in manufacturing, services, trading, or agriculture-allied (broadly any economic activity)
  • For non-individual entities, the SC/ST or woman entrepreneur must hold at least 51% shareholding and controlling interest
  • Not a defaulter to any bank or financial institution

How to apply

  1. Register on the Stand-Up India portal (standupmitra.in) or approach any scheduled commercial bank branch directly
  2. Prepare project report covering business activity, costing, financial projections, and 85:15 funding plan
  3. Submit application with KYC, caste certificate (for SC/ST), and project documents
  4. Branch processes the application; CGFSI guarantee is invoked for collateral coverage
  5. Loan sanctioned and disbursed; 18-month moratorium begins, after which repayment starts

Frequently asked

Who is eligible for Stand-Up India?
SC, ST, or woman entrepreneur aged 18 or above, starting a greenfield enterprise (first-time entrepreneur in mfg/services/trading/agri-allied). For non-individual entities, the SC/ST/woman entrepreneur must hold at least 51% shareholding.
How much can I borrow?
₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore composite loan (term + working capital), covering up to 85% of project cost. You bring 15% margin.
What is the interest rate?
Bank's MCLR + applicable tenor premium + up to 3% (regulated cap). Effective rate depends on the bank and the borrower profile.
Can I combine Stand-Up India with other schemes?
CGFSI guarantee (similar to CGTMSE) is built-in. Generally cannot be combined with PMEGP or another self-employment-loan scheme for the same unit. Stand-Up India + state subsidy on the same project may be possible — confirm with the lender.
