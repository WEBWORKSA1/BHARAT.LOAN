Stand-Up India: ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore loan for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs

Stand-Up India · Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance · Operated through SIDBI's Stand-Up Mitra portal + every scheduled commercial bank branch

Stand-Up India is the central government's targeted credit scheme to put Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe and women entrepreneurs into business ownership — a ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore composite loan available at every scheduled commercial bank branch in India, CGFSI-guaranteed, for greenfield enterprises across manufacturing, services, trading and allied agriculture.

Quick eligibility read

If you're a Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe or woman entrepreneur, 18+, starting a brand-new business in manufacturing, services, trading or allied agriculture, you can apply for a composite loan of ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore from any scheduled commercial bank branch — covering up to 85% of project cost.

What you can get
A composite loan of ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore bundling term loan and working capital into a single facility. Bank lends up to 85% of project cost; the entrepreneur brings a 15% margin contribution. Interest rate is bank's MCLR + applicable tenor premium + up to 3% (regulated cap). Tenure up to 7 years including an 18-month moratorium — the moratorium is significant because it lets the unit reach operational break-even before EMI burden kicks in.
The Credit Guarantee Fund for Stand-Up India (CGFSI) provides collateral guarantee similar in spirit to CGTMSE — the borrower is not required to pledge personal property. Stand-Up India is delivered through every scheduled commercial bank branch in India (~1.25 lakh branches); the original scheme target was at least one SC/ST and one woman borrower per branch. Publicly-reported scheme data indicates substantial cumulative take-up since 2016 launch, with over 2 lakh sanctioned loan accounts aggregating well above ₹50,000 crore.

Activities covered

Stand-Up India is sector-agnostic across four broad activity buckets. The bank's appetite, project structure and working-capital sizing differ materially by bucket — pick deliberately.

Activity bucketTypical examplesNotes
ManufacturingFood processing, garmenting, light engineering, packaging, plastics, FMCG contract manufacturingLargest share of historical approvals. Term loan ratio typically 60-70%.
ServicesEducation, healthcare clinics, hospitality, IT services, beauty & wellness, logistics, F&B outletsLower capex, faster ramp. Bank prefers franchise / proven-model service businesses.

Greenfield-only: the loan must be for a first-time venture by the applicant in that specific activity. Buying out an existing unit, recapitalising a current business, or expanding a unit you already run does not qualify.

Who it's for

First-time entrepreneurs who fall into one of three demographic categories:

  • Scheduled Caste applicants — caste certificate from the relevant state authority is mandatory documentation
  • Scheduled Tribe applicants — caste/tribe certificate from the relevant state authority is mandatory documentation
  • Women applicants — no caste/category certificate required; identity proof and the greenfield-status declaration suffice

If you're setting up a non-individual entity (LLP, Partnership, Pvt Ltd) — at least one SC/ST or woman entrepreneur must hold a minimum 51% shareholding and controlling stake (the controlling-stake requirement is enforced; nominee or proxy shareholdings will fail diligence). This structure rule is the single biggest source of declined Stand-Up India files filed by company-form applicants.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above
  • Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or Woman entrepreneur
  • Greenfield enterprise only — first-time venture by the applicant in that specific activity
  • Activity in manufacturing, services, trading, or agriculture-allied (excluded: pure crop cultivation)
  • For non-individual entities — minimum 51% shareholding and controlling stake held by the SC/ST or woman entrepreneur
  • Margin contribution of at least 15% of project cost from the entrepreneur (can be partially met by convergence support from central / state schemes)
  • Not a defaulter to any bank or financial institution
  • CIBIL / credit score consistent with lender's individual policy (varies by bank)

Which banks actually deliver?

Stand-Up India is technically available at every scheduled commercial bank branch — but branch-level willingness varies sharply. The scheme is a lender-side obligation, not an entitlement; that means execution depends on which institution's file you sit in.

Strongest delivery
Public sector banks
SBI, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, PNB, Union Bank — explicit branch-level Stand-Up India targets, dedicated nodal officers, and the cleanest CGFSI workflows. This is where the bulk of Stand-Up India sanctions actually happen.
Workable
Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)
Reasonable for sub-₹25L tickets in semi-urban and rural districts. Slower turnaround but high local approval rates when the applicant's network is in the bank's catchment.
Avoid
Private sector banks
HDFC, ICICI, Axis and similar are technically eligible lenders but routinely deflect Stand-Up India applicants into their own standard unsecured MSME products at higher rates and without the CGFSI guarantee. Workable as a last resort, not as first choice.
Patchy
Small Finance Banks
Limited Stand-Up India volume despite being eligible. Their core book is different and Stand-Up India workflows are not built out at most SFBs.

Bank choice is the single highest-leverage decision in the application. The wrong branch will spend weeks pushing your file to a different product.

How to apply

  1. Register on the Stand-Up Mitra portal (standupmitra.in) — links you to handholding agencies (DICs, NABARD partners, MSME-DI) that prepare the project report at no cost
  2. Prepare detailed project report covering business activity, costing, financial projections, and 85:15 funding structure
  3. Select bank deliberately — public sector branch with documented Stand-Up India track record beats nearest branch by a wide margin
  4. Submit application with Aadhaar/KYC, caste certificate (SC/ST only), project report, and bank statements
  5. Bank processes application; CGFSI guarantee is invoked for collateral coverage; the branch's nodal officer typically owns the file
  6. Loan sanctioned and disbursed; 18-month moratorium begins from disbursement, after which EMI starts

Frequently asked

Who is eligible for Stand-Up India?
SC, ST, or woman entrepreneur aged 18 or above, starting a greenfield enterprise (first-time venture in manufacturing, services, trading or agriculture-allied). For non-individual entities, the SC/ST/woman entrepreneur must hold at least 51% shareholding and controlling stake.
How much can I borrow?
₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore composite loan (term + working capital bundled), covering up to 85% of project cost. You bring a 15% margin contribution.
Which banks deliver Stand-Up India most reliably?
Public sector banks — SBI, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, PNB, Union Bank — have the strongest delivery track record with explicit branch-level Stand-Up India targets. Private banks routinely deflect applicants to their standard MSME products. RRBs work for smaller-ticket cases in semi-urban districts.
Do SC, ST, and women applicants need the same documents?
Nearly identical, with one key difference: caste certificate is mandatory for SC and ST applicants but not required for women applicants. All three categories need Aadhaar, KYC, project report, bank statements, and greenfield-status proof.
What is the interest rate?
Bank's MCLR + applicable tenor premium + up to 3% (regulated cap). The cap keeps Stand-Up India materially below comparable unsecured MSME loans — but the actual effective rate varies by lender and borrower profile.
What's the most common application failure?
Two failures dominate. First — non-individual applicants who don't structurally satisfy the 51% shareholding-and-controlling-stake rule (nominee or proxy structures fail diligence). Second — landing at a branch that doesn't actively process Stand-Up India and watching the file drift for months before being deflected to a standard product.
Can I combine Stand-Up India with other schemes?
CGFSI guarantee is built-in (similar to CGTMSE). Cannot be combined with PMEGP or another central self-employment-loan scheme for the same unit. State-level subsidies (interest subvention, stamp duty exemption) typically stack — confirm with the lender at sanction stage.
