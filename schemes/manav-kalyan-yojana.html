Manav Kalyan & Manav Garima Yojana: free trade toolkits in Gujarat

Manav Kalyan Yojana · Commissioner of Cottage & Rural Industries · e-Kutir portal | Manav Garima Yojana · SC Welfare, Gujarat

Free trade toolkits for low-income artisans, workers and small vendors in Gujarat — plus paid entrepreneurship training — so you can start earning on your own.

Quick eligibility read

Low-income artisans, labourers and small vendors aged 18–60 in Gujarat who need tools for a trade; SC applicants can also use Manav Garima Yojana.

What you can get
A free toolkit for your trade, delivered as an e-voucher, plus optional entrepreneurship training with a ₹500 daily stipend.
Toolkits are capped per trade by the annual GR. Selection is by online draw, so apply as soon as the window opens.

Two toolkit schemes

SchemeWhoHow it works
Manav Kalyan YojanaLow-income artisans, labourers and small vendors aged 18–60 (income criteria in the current GR; relaxed for SC/ST, most-backward and nomadic communities)Online application on e-Kutir; beneficiaries selected by online draw; toolkit given as an e-voucher redeemable at approved dealers; optional 5-day entrepreneurship training with ₹500 a day stipend
Manav Garima YojanaScheduled Caste applicants within the income ceilingFree trade toolkit; apply on e-Samaj Kalyan; verified by the Mamlatdar and approved by the SC Welfare department

Trades include dairy-product sales, plumbing, embroidery, centering work, beauty parlour, electrical-appliance repair, papad and pickle making, vehicle servicing and puncture-repair kits; the list and per-toolkit limits are set by GR each year.

Who it’s for

Self-employed workers and would-be micro-entrepreneurs — plumbers, beauticians, tailors, embroiderers, repair technicians, small food producers and vendors.

Eligibility

  • Resident of Gujarat aged 18–60
  • Within the income criteria in the current GR (BPL card or income certificate; relaxed for SC/ST and most-backward communities)
  • Choose one trade from the notified list
  • Not received the same toolkit earlier
  • Manav Garima: Scheduled Caste applicant with caste certificate

How to apply

  1. Register on the e-Kutir portal with Aadhaar and mobile number
  2. Complete your profile and choose the scheme and trade
  3. Upload documents and submit; note the application number
  4. Wait for the online draw and approval
  5. Redeem the e-voucher at an approved dealer and attend the training

Documents you’ll need

Aadhaar cardIncome certificate or BPL proofCaste certificate (if applicable)Proof of ageBank passbookPassport-size photo

Frequently asked

What do I get under Manav Kalyan Yojana?
A free toolkit for your trade — such as a beauty-parlour kit, plumbing tools, an embroidery machine or a vehicle-servicing kit — given as an e-voucher you redeem at an approved dealer. Since 2024-25 selected beneficiaries also get five days of entrepreneurship training with a ₹500 daily stipend.
How are beneficiaries chosen?
Applications are made online on the e-Kutir portal and beneficiaries are picked by an online draw.
What is Manav Garima Yojana?
A similar free-toolkit scheme for Scheduled Caste applicants, run by the SC Welfare department through the e-Samaj Kalyan portal.
Who runs Manav Kalyan Yojana?
The Commissioner of Cottage & Rural Industries under Gujarat’s Industries & Mines Department; District Industries Centres help applicants.
