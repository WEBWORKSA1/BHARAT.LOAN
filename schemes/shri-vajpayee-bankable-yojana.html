Shri Vajpayee Bankable Yojana: loan up to ₹25 lakh with up to 40% subsidy

Shri Vajpayee Bankable Yojana · Commissioner of Cottage & Rural Industries · District Industries Centres, Gujarat

Gujarat’s own self-employment scheme — a bank loan of up to ₹25 lakh for a small industry, service or trade business, with 20% to 40% of it paid as subsidy (maximum ₹3.75 lakh). No income limit.

Loan
Up to ₹25 lakh
Subsidy
20%–40%, max ₹3.75 lakh

Quick eligibility read

Gujarat resident aged 18–65 with at least Class 4 schooling, relevant training or experience, or a hereditary craft — starting a small industry, service or trade unit with a bank loan.

What you can get
A bank loan of up to ₹25 lakh with a subsidy of 20%–25% (general) or 30%–40% (SC/ST, women, ex-servicemen, persons with disabilities), capped at ₹3.75 lakh.
Higher rates apply in rural areas. The subsidy is adjusted against the loan, so you repay less.

General20%25%
SC/ST, women, ex-servicemen, persons with disabilities (40%+)30%40%

Subsidy is capped at ₹3.75 lakh and is credited to the loan account. Revised in March 2026 (loan limit raised from ₹8 lakh to ₹25 lakh; subsidy cap from ₹1.25 lakh to ₹3.75 lakh). About 1.42 lakh beneficiaries received ₹1,243 crore over the five years before the revision.

Who it’s for

Unemployed youth, artisans, women and first-generation entrepreneurs in Gujarat starting small manufacturing, service or trading businesses — shops, workshops, repair units, tailoring, food businesses and similar.

Eligibility

  • Resident of Gujarat
  • Aged 18 to 65 years
  • At least Class 4 pass, or relevant training / experience, or a hereditary craftsperson
  • No family-income ceiling
  • Activity in the industry, service or trade (business) sector
  • Not using another subsidy-linked self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply

  1. Prepare a simple project report and get quotations for equipment
  2. Apply on the Bankable Loan Portal (blp.gujarat.gov.in) or at your District Industries Centre
  3. DIC scrutinises the application and recommends it to the bank
  4. The bank sanctions the loan; subsidy is credited to the loan account
  5. Start the business and keep it running through the lock-in period

Documents you’ll need

Aadhaar cardSchool leaving certificate / education proofTraining or experience certificate (if applicable)Caste certificate (SC/ST)Project report and quotationsBusiness address proofPassport-size photosBank passbook

Frequently asked

How much subsidy does Shri Vajpayee Bankable Yojana give?
20% of the loan in urban areas and 25% in rural areas for general applicants; 30% (urban) and 40% (rural) for SC/ST applicants, women, ex-servicemen and persons with 40% or more disability — capped at ₹3.75 lakh.
What is the maximum loan?
₹25 lakh, after the March 2026 revision. The earlier limit was ₹8 lakh with a ₹1.25 lakh subsidy cap.
Is there an income limit?
No. Any Gujarat resident aged 18–65 who meets the education, training, experience or hereditary-craft criterion can apply.
Can I take PMEGP and SVBY for the same unit?
No — choose one subsidy-linked self-employment scheme for a unit. SVBY often suits smaller trade and service units; PMEGP allows bigger manufacturing projects. We compare both for your plan.
Where do I apply?
Online on Gujarat’s Bankable Loan Portal (blp.gujarat.gov.in) or through your District Industries Centre, which scrutinises the file and recommends it to the bank.
Where an advisor helps

We check whether SVBY or PMEGP gives you more money for your project, prepare a bank-ready project report, and follow the file through DIC and the bank.
