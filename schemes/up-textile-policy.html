Quick eligibility read

New textile / garmenting unit in Uttar Pradesh, or an existing unit expanding by >25% of fixed capital and capacity.

What you can get
Fiscal incentives for new textile and garmenting units, and for existing units undertaking expansion / diversification with an investment of more than 25% of existing gross fixed capital and a capacity increase of at least 25%. The policy targets ₹10,000 crore of fresh investment over five years across UP's established textile districts (Varanasi, Bhadohi, Gautam Budh Nagar, Kanpur, Lucknow, Sitapur), known for textile and carpet exports.
Notified 2022 by the UP Industries Department. Specific subsidy rates and caps are set in the policy and its annexures and are revised periodically — the exact per-component quantum should be confirmed for your district and sub-sector. Several UP textile districts overlap with their ODOP product (e.g. Bhadohi carpets, Varanasi silk), so ODOP and the textile policy can be evaluated together.

Who it's for

Textile and garmenting manufacturers in Uttar Pradesh — apparel, made-ups, carpets, silk and allied textile activities — setting up new units or undertaking qualifying expansion.

Eligibility

  • Textile / garmenting unit located in Uttar Pradesh
  • New unit, or existing unit expanding by more than 25% of existing gross fixed capital with a minimum 25% capacity increase
  • Investment in eligible textile / garmenting activity
  • Compliance with the 2022 policy's registration and sector conditions

How to apply

  1. Confirm your textile sub-sector and (for expansion) the >25% capital and capacity thresholds
  2. Arrange eligible financing for the project
  3. Apply through the UP Directorate of Industries / Invest UP channel
  4. Claim the policy's incentives per the sanctioned terms

Documents you'll need

Udyam / unit registrationUP unit and land documentsTerm loan sanction and machinery invoicesProject report (with expansion calculation, if applicable)District / sub-sector classification

Frequently asked

Which districts does this target?
UP's established textile belt — Varanasi, Bhadohi, Kanpur, Lucknow, Sitapur, Gautam Budh Nagar — known for textiles and carpet exports, though the policy applies statewide.
Can an existing unit qualify?
Yes — via expansion/diversification, provided you invest more than 25% of existing gross fixed capital and raise capacity by at least 25%.
Does this stack with ODOP?
Several textile districts have a textile ODOP product (Bhadohi carpets, Varanasi silk), so the two can often be evaluated together — we model the combined position.
