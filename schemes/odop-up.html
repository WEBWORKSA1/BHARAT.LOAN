ODOP (One District One Product)

One District One Product — Financing & Margin-Money Scheme · Est. 2018 · Directorate of Industries / ODOP Cell, UP

Quick eligibility read

18+ applicant setting up an industry, service or business unit in the designated ODOP product of their UP district. No educational-qualification bar.

What you can get
Margin-money financial grant of 25% of project cost up to ₹6.25 lakh (whichever is less) for projects up to ₹25 lakh; tiered slabs apply above ₹25 lakh and up to ₹50 lakh / ₹150 lakh. Each of UP's 75 districts has a designated product (e.g. Bhadohi carpets, Kanpur leather, Moradabad brass, Varanasi silk & saris, Siddharthnagar Kalanamak rice, Agra leather footwear, Firozabad glass) — the unit must work in that district's identified ODOP product.
Launched 2018 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to revive indigenous district products. ODOP coordinates with Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarozgar Yojana and Vishwakarma Shram Samman for the financing flow, and partners with marketplaces (e.g. Amazon) for distribution. Margin-money slabs and the per-district product list are notified by the ODOP cell.

Who it's for

Entrepreneurs, artisans and micro-units in any of UP's 75 districts who manufacture, service or trade in that district's officially designated ODOP product.

Eligibility

  • Applicant aged 18 or above (no educational-qualification requirement)
  • Unit operates in the ODOP product identified for that specific district
  • Industry, service or business sector all eligible
  • Not a defaulter of any bank / financial / government institution
  • Not simultaneously availing PMEGP / CM self-employment / other central or state self-employment scheme for the same unit

How to apply

  1. Confirm your district's designated ODOP product and that your unit matches it
  2. Prepare a project report for the ODOP product
  3. Apply through the ODOP / District Industries Centre channel (often routed via CMYSY)
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions; margin-money grant is credited against the loan

Frequently asked

What is my district's product?
Each of UP's 75 districts has one designated product — Bhadohi carpets, Kanpur leather, Moradabad brass, Varanasi silk, Firozabad glass, and so on. Eligibility is tied to that specific product. We confirm yours.
How much margin money do I get?
Up to 25% of project cost capped at ₹6.25 lakh for projects up to ₹25 lakh, with tiered slabs above that. Category applicants may get an enhanced rate.
Can I combine ODOP with PMEGP?
Generally not for the same unit — you can't double-dip across self-employment schemes. The right single route depends on your product, district and project size.
