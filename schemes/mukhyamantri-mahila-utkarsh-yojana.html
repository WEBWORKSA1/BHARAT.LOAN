Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana: interest-free ₹1 lakh loans for women’s groups

Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana (MMUY) · Gujarat Livelihood Promotion Company & Gujarat Urban Livelihood Mission

Interest-free loans of up to ₹1 lakh for each group of 10 women in Gujarat, rural or urban, to start or expand small businesses — the state pays the interest.

Loan
Up to ₹1 lakh per group
Interest
0% for the group
Group size
10 women
Coverage
Rural + urban

Quick eligibility read

A group of 10 women organised as a self-help or joint liability & earning group in Gujarat, rural or urban, wanting working capital for small businesses.

What you can get
An interest-free loan of up to ₹1 lakh per 10-member women’s group; the state reimburses the interest to the lender.
Repay on time to keep the loan interest-free. Groups can graduate to larger bank linkages and to individual schemes like SVBY or Mudra.

Who it’s for

Women’s self-help groups and joint liability groups — home-based food businesses, tailoring, dairy, small retail, handicrafts and other livelihood activities.

Eligibility

  • Group of 10 women
  • Rural groups through the Gujarat Livelihood Promotion Company (Sakhi Mandal)
  • Urban groups through the Gujarat Urban Livelihood Mission
  • Group registered and linked to a bank or cooperative lender
  • Loan used for income-generating activity

How to apply

  1. Form or join a 10-member women’s group
  2. Register with your Sakhi Mandal / taluka livelihood office (rural) or the municipal urban livelihood office (urban)
  3. Apply through the group with the MMUY form
  4. The lender sanctions the loan; the state pays the interest
  5. Repay instalments on time to keep the interest-free benefit

Documents you’ll need

Group resolutionMembers’ Aadhaar cardsGroup bank-account detailsMembers’ photosBusiness activity details

Frequently asked

What is Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana?
A Gujarat scheme that gives interest-free loans of up to ₹1 lakh to groups of 10 women so they can start or grow small businesses. The state pays the interest.
Who can apply?
Women’s self-help groups / joint liability and earning groups of 10 members — rural groups through the Gujarat Livelihood Promotion Company (Sakhi Mandal / Mission Mangalam) and urban groups through the Gujarat Urban Livelihood Mission.
Is it really interest-free?
Yes, for the borrowing group — the government reimburses the interest to the lending bank or cooperative when the group repays on time.
Where do I apply?
Through your Sakhi Mandal / taluka livelihood office (rural) or your municipal urban livelihood mission office (urban). The official portal is mmuy.gujarat.gov.in.
Where an advisor helps

We help women’s groups choose an activity, prepare the paperwork and plan the next step up — individual loans under SVBY, Mudra or Stand-Up India.
