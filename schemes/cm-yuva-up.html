CM-YUVA (Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Vikas Abhiyan)

Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Vikas Abhiyan — Interest-Free Youth Enterprise Loan · Est. 2024 · Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP

UP's flagship youth scheme — a 0% interest, collateral-free loan up to ₹5 lakh, with a 10% subsidy on timely repayment and the state covering your CGTMSE cost.

Quick eligibility read

UP resident aged 21–40, 8th-pass minimum, holding a skill/training certificate (ODOP / Vishwakarma / UPSDM), starting a new micro manufacturing or service unit.

What you can get
Interest-free (0%) composite loan — term loan plus working capital — for a project cost up to ₹5 lakh in Phase 1 (loan component up to ₹4.5 lakh). No collateral or third-party guarantee; the state government bears the CGTMSE coverage cost. 4-year tenure including a 6-month moratorium. On full and timely repayment, an additional 10% of project cost is granted as subsidy. Phase 2 extends financing up to ₹10 lakh for units that completed Phase 1.
Launched 2024 by the Directorate of Industries & Enterprise Promotion, UP, with a ₹1,000 crore Budget 2024-25 allocation. Target: 1 lakh youth units per year, 10 lakh units over 10 years. The 0% interest and CGTMSE cost are borne by the state for eligible Phase 1 beneficiaries; a personal promoter guarantee is still required.

Who it's for

Educated, skilled young residents of Uttar Pradesh (21–40) setting up a new manufacturing or service micro-enterprise, who have completed a recognised government skill-training programme.

Eligibility

  • Permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh
  • Aged 21–40 years
  • At least 8th-standard pass (intermediate preferred)
  • Holds a skill certificate / diploma or trained under a government scheme (Vishwakarma Shram Samman, ODOP Training & Toolkit, UPSDM, SC/ST/OBC training)
  • Setting up a new unit — not already benefiting from another self-employment scheme for the same unit
  • Udyam registration required

How to apply

  1. Register on the CM-YUVA portal with Aadhaar OTP verification
  2. Complete the application — personal, business and project details
  3. Upload identity, residence, skill certificate, bank and project-report documents
  4. Bank and state officials verify the application and financials
  5. On approval the bank disburses the 0% composite loan; repay over 4 years to unlock the +10% subsidy

Documents you'll need

Aadhaar (with OTP verification on the portal)UP residence certificateSkill / training certificateEducational qualification proofProject reportBank passbook and Udyam registration

Frequently asked

Is it really 0% interest?
Yes — for eligible Phase 1 beneficiaries the interest is fully borne by the state, and the loan is collateral-free with CGTMSE cost also covered by UP. A personal promoter guarantee is still required.
What is the 10% bonus?
On full and timely repayment within the 4-year tenure, the government grants an additional 10% of project cost as a subsidy — a strong reason to stay current.
Do I need prior training?
Yes — you must hold a skill certificate or have trained under a recognised government programme (ODOP, Vishwakarma, UPSDM). This is the most common eligibility gate.
