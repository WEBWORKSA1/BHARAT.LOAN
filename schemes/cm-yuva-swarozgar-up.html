Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarozgar Yojana (CMYSY)

UP Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarozgar Yojana — Margin-Money Self-Employment Scheme · Directorate of Industries, UP

Margin-money subsidy for new UP enterprises — up to ₹6.25 lakh (industry) / ₹2.50 lakh (service), on projects up to ₹25 lakh.

Quick eligibility read

UP resident aged 18–40, high-school pass, starting a new manufacturing (≤₹25L) or service (≤₹10L) unit, not a prior self-employment-scheme beneficiary.

What you can get
Margin-money subsidy of 25% of total project cost — capped at ₹6.25 lakh for the industry/manufacturing sector and ₹2.50 lakh for the service sector. Project cost up to ₹25 lakh (manufacturing) / ₹10 lakh (service). Category applicants (SC/ST/OBC, women, ex-servicemen, Divyang, and NE/hill/border areas) get an enhanced subsidy rate vs the general category.
Delivered by the UP Directorate of Industries through the District Industries Centre. One of UP's highest-volume schemes — ₹940.86 crore disbursed to 37,280 beneficiaries historically. Cannot be combined with PMEGP or other central/state self-employment schemes for the same unit.

Who it's for

Unemployed / first-venture youth of Uttar Pradesh (18–40) setting up a new manufacturing or service enterprise who haven't taken benefits under PMEGP or another self-employment scheme.

Eligibility

  • Resident of Uttar Pradesh aged 18–40
  • Minimum high-school (10th) pass
  • Not a defaulter of any nationalised bank / financial / government institution
  • Has not availed PMEGP, PM Rozgar Yojana or any other central/state self-employment scheme
  • New unit — manufacturing project ≤ ₹25 lakh or service project ≤ ₹10 lakh

How to apply

  1. Confirm eligibility and prepare a project report within the sector cap
  2. Apply through the UP Directorate of Industries / District Industries Centre portal
  3. DIC reviews and forwards to the financing bank
  4. Bank sanctions; the 25% margin-money subsidy is credited against the loan

Documents you'll need

Aadhaar and KYCUP residence proofEducational qualification (10th pass)Project reportBank account detailsCaste / category certificate (for enhanced rate, if applicable)

Frequently asked

How is this different from CM-YUVA?
CMYSY is a margin-money subsidy (25%) on a normal bank loan; CM-YUVA is a fully interest-free loan for younger applicants. Different age bands, different mechanics — we match you to the better one.
Do category applicants get more?
Yes — SC/ST/OBC, women, ex-servicemen, Divyang and NE/hill/border-area applicants get an enhanced subsidy rate over the general category.
