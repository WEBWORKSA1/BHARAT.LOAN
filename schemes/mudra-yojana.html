PM Mudra Yojana

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) · Est. 2015 · MUDRA / Member Lending Institutions

Quick eligibility read

Any 18+ individual or micro-enterprise with a viable business plan in manufacturing, trading, services or allied-agri. Not for direct crop agriculture or large corporates.

What you can get
Collateral-free loans on four tiers: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,001–₹5L), Tarun (₹5L–₹10L), and Tarun Plus (₹10L–₹20L, introduced via Budget 2024-25 for those who repaid a prior Tarun loan).
No direct subsidy under core PMMY; interest is bank MCLR-based, not a fixed government rate. A 2% interest subvention applied to Shishu loans for prompt repayment in some windows — confirm current. Loans are collateral-free and guarantor-free.

Who it's for

Non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises — manufacturing, trading, services, and allied agricultural activities (dairy, poultry, food processing). New and existing both.

Eligibility

  • Applicant typically 18–65
  • A viable business plan or an existing small business
  • Business in manufacturing, trading, services, or allied-agri (not direct crop/farm loans)
  • CIBIL score not mandatory for Shishu; 650+ generally preferred for Kishore/Tarun
  • Tarun Plus specifically requires prior successful repayment of a Tarun loan

How to apply

  1. Pick the tier (Shishu/Kishore/Tarun/Tarun Plus) matching your funding need
  2. Approach a MUDRA-approved bank, NBFC, RRB or MFI — or apply via the JanSamarth portal
  3. Submit the application with business details and documents
  4. Bank verifies documents and business plan; on approval funds are disbursed (a MUDRA card may be issued)

Frequently asked

Is there a subsidy on Mudra?
Not directly under core PMMY. Relief comes via linked schemes (e.g. CLCSS) or limited interest-subvention windows. The core benefit is collateral-free access.
Do I need collateral or a guarantor?
No — Mudra loans are collateral-free and guarantor-free, which is the whole point of the scheme.
Is the ₹10 lakh limit still current?
The limit was raised to ₹20 lakh via the Tarun Plus tier (Budget 2024-25). Many older guides still say ₹10 lakh.
