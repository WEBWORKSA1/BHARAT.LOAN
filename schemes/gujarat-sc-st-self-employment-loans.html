SC/ST self-employment loans in Gujarat: 1–5% loans, toolkits & interest subsidy

Quick eligibility read

Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe resident of Gujarat starting or growing a small business; corporation loans are income-capped.

What you can get
Concessional SC loans at 1%–5%, free trade toolkits, a 6% interest subsidy on ST self-employment loans up to ₹5 lakh — and larger MSME subsidies under Ambedkar Udhyog Uday.
Pick the route by project size: toolkits and corporation loans for small starts, SVBY for up to ₹25 lakh, Ambedkar Udhyog Uday and Stand-Up India for larger units.

Who it’s for

SC and ST entrepreneurs, artisans and self-employed workers across Gujarat — including tribal-area residents.

Eligibility

  • Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe certificate issued in Gujarat
  • Resident of Gujarat
  • Income within the scheme limit (₹3 lakh family income for GSCDC loans)
  • ST interest subsidy: resident of a tribal area, loan from a bank for self-employment
  • Viable business plan and quotations

How to apply

  1. Pick the scheme that matches your project size
  2. SC: register on e-Samaj Kalyan and apply online; ST: apply to the Tribal Development Department / Gujarat Tribal Development Corporation
  3. Submit caste, income and business documents
  4. District office verifies and sanctions
  5. Loan or subsidy is released to your bank account

Frequently asked

What loans does the Gujarat SC Development Corporation give?
Self-employment loans financed with the National Scheduled Castes Finance & Development Corporation (NSFDC) at concessional interest of 1%–5%, for SC applicants with family income up to ₹3 lakh.
Is there a scheme for tribal entrepreneurs?
Yes — the Tribal Development Department gives a 6% interest subsidy on bank loans of up to ₹5 lakh for self-employment to ST applicants in tribal areas, and the Gujarat Tribal Development Corporation runs its own loan schemes.
Which is better — SVBY or a corporation loan?
SVBY gives SC/ST applicants a 30%–40% subsidy on bank loans up to ₹25 lakh; corporation loans give very low interest on smaller amounts. Bigger projects should look at Dr. Ambedkar Udhyog Uday and Stand-Up India.
Where do I apply?
SC schemes on the e-Samaj Kalyan portal (esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in); ST schemes through the Tribal Development Department and the Gujarat Tribal Development Corporation (adijatinigam.gujarat.gov.in).
