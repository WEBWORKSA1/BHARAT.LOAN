PMFME: PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises

Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises Scheme · Est. 2020 · Ministry of Food Processing Industries · ₹10,000 crore outlay over 5 years

MoFPI's flagship scheme to formalise India's vast unorganised food-processing micro-enterprise sector — a 35% credit-linked capital subsidy up to ₹10 lakh per unit, plus separate seed-capital and infrastructure grants for SHGs, FPOs and cooperatives, with strong ODOP preference.

Quick eligibility read

Existing or new micro food processing enterprise (individual / partnership / FPO / SHG / NGO / Cooperative / Producer Company) — with strong preference for units producing the district's ODOP food product, but non-ODOP units also eligible.

What you can get
A 35% credit-linked capital subsidy on project cost capped at ₹10 lakh per micro food processing unit. SHGs get a dedicated rail with ₹4 lakh per member as seed capital (max ₹40 lakh per group) for working capital and small tools. FPOs, Cooperatives and Producer Companies get up to 35% grant on common infrastructure (grading, sorting, processing, warehousing, packaging) capped at ₹3 crore. Branding and marketing support of up to 50% of expenditure for FPOs/SHGs/Cooperatives. Capacity-building (training) and hand-holding (DPR preparation via District Resource Persons) are fully funded. Promoter contribution must be at least 10% of project cost.
Launched June 2020 by the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) with a ₹10,000 crore outlay over 5 years (2020-21 to 2024-25, extended). Cost-shared 60:40 between Centre and States (90:10 for NE & Himalayan states, 60:40 for UTs with legislature, 100% Centre for UTs without). Target: formalise 2,00,000 micro food processing units across the country.

Who it's for

Existing or new micro food processing enterprises across India — grain milling, oil pressing, fruit and vegetable processing, dairy, marine and meat processing, bakery, confectionery, beverages, spice processing, millet products, organic and ethnic foods. Eligible applicant types span individuals, partnership firms, Pvt Ltd companies, FPOs, SHGs, NGOs, Cooperatives and Producer Companies — with strong preference for units making their district's officially designated ODOP food product.

Eligibility

  • Micro food processing enterprise (within the MSME micro-unit definition — P&M investment up to ₹1 Cr, turnover up to ₹5 Cr)
  • Existing or new unit — greenfield, upgrade, and expansion are all eligible
  • Applicant is at least 18 years old; one applicant per family for the individual rail
  • Promoter contribution of at least 10% of project cost
  • Preference (but not compulsory) for units producing the district's notified ODOP food product
  • Aadhaar mandatory; Indian citizen
  • Loan-linked — applicant must be willing to take a bank loan; the 35% subsidy is credited back against the loan

How to apply

  1. Confirm your district's notified ODOP food product (preference, not mandatory)
  2. Register on the PMFME portal (pmfme.mofpi.gov.in) with Aadhaar
  3. Engage with a District Resource Person (DRP) for DPR preparation — fully funded by the scheme
  4. Submit the application with project plan, financials and Aadhaar/KYC
  5. State Nodal Agency reviews; bank appraises and sanctions the loan
  6. On disbursement and milestones, the 35% subsidy (capped at ₹10L) is credited against the loan account

Frequently asked

Do I have to make my district's ODOP food product?
No — ODOP units get preference and faster processing, but non-ODOP micro food processing units are also eligible. Each state has notified district-level ODOP food products (e.g. mango pulp, makhana, millet, dairy, marine), but the scheme accepts other categories too.
How is PMFME different from PMEGP?
PMEGP is a general manufacturing/service self-employment subsidy (15-35% by category and area). PMFME is food-processing specific, with a higher 35% rate up to ₹10 lakh, plus separate SHG and FPO/Cooperative rails that PMEGP doesn't have. For a food unit, PMFME is almost always richer.
Can SHGs apply for PMFME?
Yes — SHGs get a dedicated track with ₹4 lakh per member (max ₹40 lakh per group) as seed capital for working capital and small tools, on top of the standard 35% capital subsidy on the unit itself.
What about existing food processing units?
Existing micro units upgrading machinery or expanding capacity are explicitly eligible — not just greenfield. The 10% promoter contribution rule still applies.
Can I stack PMFME with other schemes?
Generally no — you can't double-dip across central self-employment subsidies (PMEGP, PMFME, Stand-Up India) for the same unit. PMFME often stacks fine with state-level interest subsidies and stamp-duty exemptions, but the central self-employment rails are mutually exclusive.
