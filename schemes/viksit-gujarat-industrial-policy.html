Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026: capital, interest & power incentives

Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 · Effective 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2031 · Industries & Mines Department, Gujarat

Gujarat’s new umbrella incentive policy — choose your own mix of capital subsidy, 7% interest subsidy and power-tariff support, worth 15% to 50% of eligible investment depending on unit size, sector and taluka.

Quick eligibility read

New manufacturing unit — micro to ultra-mega — starting commercial production in Gujarat on or after 1 June 2026. Your taluka category (A or B), unit size and sector set the rate.

What you can get
MSMEs get a capital subsidy of 35% (Category-A taluka) or 25% (Category-B) of eligible fixed capital investment, a 7% interest subsidy for 5 years and a power-tariff subsidy — within an overall ceiling of 45% or 35% of investment.
Large units get 15%–35%, mega and ultra-mega thrust-sector projects up to 40%, and selected thrust sectors (toys, footwear, sports goods, robots, drones) up to 50%. Every eligible unit also gets EPF reimbursement and electricity-duty exemption.

Percentages are of eligible fixed capital investment (eFCI). You choose the mix of capital subsidy, 7% interest subsidy and power-tariff subsidy (₹2/unit in Category-A, ₹1/unit in Category-B) — the total cannot exceed the overall ceiling. 130 talukas are Category-A (less industrialised) and 138 are Category-B; the taluka list was notified by GR in September 2026.

Other components

  • EPF reimbursement: 100% of the employer’s contribution for new employees for 5–10 years, capped at ₹1,800/month (men), ₹2,500 (women) and ₹3,000 (persons with disabilities)
  • Electricity duty: 100% exemption under the Gujarat Electricity Duty Act, 1958
  • Women entrepreneurs: additional 1% interest subsidy and rent assistance of 75%, up to ₹3 lakh a year for 5 years
  • SC/ST entrepreneurs: an additional 5% on the incentive ceiling (as reported at launch — confirm in the operational GR)
  • Green manufacturing: wastewater recycling 50% of project cost up to ₹75 crore (≥70% recycling); zero-liquid-discharge 40–50%; cleaner production 25–50% up to ₹1–2 crore
  • R&D centres: 25–50% capital support, ₹10,000 per researcher per month for 3 years, 75% of patent cost up to ₹15 lakh per patent
  • Selected thrust extras: patents 75% up to ₹1 crore, technology acquisition 75% up to ₹5 crore, international certification 100% up to ₹5 crore, training ₹15,000 per employee per month for 12 months
  • Workforce housing: 80% of cost, up to ₹40 crore, for dormitories and working women’s hostels
  • Startups: sustenance allowance, seed support and an extra 1% interest subsidy — see the Gujarat Startup page

Thrust sectors

Green energy (green hydrogen, electrolysers, renewable equipment, batteries, fuel cells), mobility (auto, aviation, space), capital equipment, textiles & apparel, critical minerals, metals and ceramics, chemicals, agro & food processing, healthcare (APIs, bulk drugs, medical devices), semiconductor ancillaries, nuclear power equipment, plus vehicle scrapping, e-waste and textile-waste recycling, shipping containers, heavy earth-moving equipment, sports goods, toys, footwear, robots and drones.

If you were under the 2022 Aatmanirbhar schemes

Units already in commercial production before 1 June 2026 cannot move to the new policy. Projects under implementation could opt — once and irrevocably — to stay on the 2022 scheme within six months of the GR, subject to that scheme’s production cut-off of 4 October 2027. SGST reimbursement is not available under the 2026 policy.

Who it’s for

Manufacturers of every size setting up or expanding in Gujarat — from a micro unit in a Category-A taluka to ultra-mega thrust-sector projects — plus women- and SC/ST-led enterprises, R&D centres, green-infrastructure projects and developers of worker housing.

Eligibility

  • New unit, or expansion, starting commercial production on or after 1 June 2026
  • Located in Gujarat; rates depend on the taluka category (A or B)
  • MSME by the national definition — micro up to ₹2.5 Cr, small up to ₹25 Cr, medium up to ₹125 Cr investment; large ₹125 Cr+
  • Mega and ultra-mega status only for notified thrust sectors (₹1,000 Cr+ / ₹10,000 Cr+ with employment thresholds)
  • Project registered before commercial production (or within 3 months of the GR, whichever is later)
  • Eligibility certificate applied for within 3 months of commercial production

How to apply

  1. Confirm your unit class, thrust-sector status and taluka category
  2. Register the project with the Industries Commissionerate / Investor Facilitation Portal before commercial production
  3. Choose your incentive mix — capital, interest and power — within the ceiling
  4. Start commercial production and apply for the eligibility certificate within 3 months (MSMEs typically through the District Industries Centre; the operational GR sets the exact channel)
  5. Claim each year’s disbursement — capital subsidy is phased over 1 to 12 years depending on unit class

Frequently asked

What is the Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026?
Gujarat’s new five-year industrial incentive policy, launched by Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel on 15 June 2026 and effective from 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2031. It targets ₹10 lakh crore of investment and replaces the 2022 Aatmanirbhar Gujarat incentive schemes for new units.
How much can an MSME get?
Up to 45% of eligible fixed capital investment in a Category-A taluka (35% in Category-B), as a mix of capital subsidy (35% / 25%), a 7% interest subsidy for 5 years and a power-tariff subsidy of ₹2 / ₹1 per unit for 5 years.
What does ‘Choose Your Incentive’ mean?
Instead of a fixed package, the unit picks how to take its benefit — more capital subsidy upfront, or interest and power support over time — as long as the total stays within the overall ceiling for its category and taluka.
Is SGST reimbursement still available?
No. The 2026 policy drops net-SGST reimbursement, which was central to the 2022 Aatmanirbhar schemes. Support now comes as capital, interest and power-tariff subsidies plus EPF and electricity-duty benefits.
Which taluka category am I in?
Gujarat notified 130 Category-A and 138 Category-B talukas by GR in September 2026. Category-A talukas are less industrialised and get higher rates. Your District Industries Centre can confirm the category for your plot.
Do I qualify if my unit started production in May 2026?
No. Units in commercial production before 1 June 2026 stay on the earlier scheme. Projects that were still under implementation could choose to remain on the 2022 scheme within six months of the GR.
