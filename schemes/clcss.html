CLCSS

Credit Linked Capital Subsidy Scheme · Est. 2000 · Ministry of MSME

A capital subsidy that pays back part of what you spend upgrading plant and machinery to better technology.

Quick eligibility read

Existing MSME upgrading to approved technology in a notified sub-sector, taking institutional credit for the machinery.

What you can get
15% capital subsidy on institutional credit for technology upgradation, on eligible plant & machinery investment up to ₹1 crore (subsidy ceiling and eligible sub-sectors are notified — confirm current list).
CLCSS supports upgrading to approved, better technology in notified sub-sectors. It is a capital subsidy on the machinery cost, routed through the lending bank.

Who it's for

Existing micro and small manufacturing enterprises upgrading to modern, approved technology in one of the notified sub-sectors.

Eligibility

  • Existing MSME in manufacturing (the scheme targets technology upgradation, not greenfield)
  • Investment in approved/better technology within a notified sub-sector/product
  • Financing through institutional credit (a term loan from a participating bank/FI)
  • Valid Udyam registration
  • Machinery must meet the scheme's approved-technology criteria

How to apply

  1. Confirm your upgrade falls in a notified CLCSS sub-sector with approved technology
  2. Arrange a term loan for the machinery from a participating bank/FI
  3. Apply for the capital subsidy through the bank, which routes it to the nodal agency
  4. Subsidy is credited against the loan once approved

Documents you'll need

Udyam Registration certificateTerm loan sanction from the financing bankMachinery invoices / quotations meeting approved-technology criteriaBusiness financialsApplication through the nodal bank/agency

Frequently asked

Can a new business use CLCSS?
No — CLCSS is for upgradation by existing units, not for setting up a brand-new unit. New units look to PMEGP or CGTMSE-backed credit.
Does my machinery qualify?
Only technology in the scheme's notified sub-sectors and approved-technology list qualifies. This is exactly where applications fail — verify before you buy.
Where an advisor helps
