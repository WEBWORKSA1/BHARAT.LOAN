Quick eligibility read

Existing, growing MSME with a financial track record seeking growth or machinery finance beyond micro-ticket size.

What you can get
Direct loans and refinance facilities for MSME growth — capacity expansion, machinery, working capital — with various dedicated lending products through SIDBI.
SIDBI is the apex MSME development institution; it lends directly and refinances other lenders. Specific product terms, rates and ticket sizes vary by SIDBI scheme — confirm the current product fit.

Who it's for

Established and growing MSMEs needing larger or specialised finance for expansion, modernisation, or working capital, beyond the micro-loan tiers.

Eligibility

  • Existing MSME with an operating and financial track record
  • A defined growth, machinery, or working-capital need
  • Meets the specific SIDBI product's eligibility and documentation
  • Valid business registration / Udyam

How to apply

  1. Identify the SIDBI product matching your need (direct loan vs refinance vs specialised scheme)
  2. Approach SIDBI or a partner channel with your financials and project details
  3. Appraisal of the proposal against the product's criteria
  4. Sanction and disbursement per the product terms

Frequently asked

Is SIDBI only for very large MSMEs?
No — SIDBI has a range of products, but it tends to suit growing units needing larger or specialised finance beyond the micro tiers.
Direct loan vs refinance — what's the difference?
Direct means SIDBI lends to you; refinance means SIDBI funds another lender who lends to you. The right route depends on your size and product fit.
