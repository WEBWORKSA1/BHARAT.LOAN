Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Udhyog Uday Yojana: subsidy for SC/ST-owned MSMEs

Bharat Ratna Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Udhyog Uday Yojana · Industries Commissionerate · District Industries Centres, Gujarat

Gujarat’s dedicated package for SC/ST entrepreneurs — a capital subsidy of up to 25% of the term loan (max ₹35 lakh) plus a 6%–8% interest subsidy for five years, with 1% extra for founders under 35.

Quick eligibility read

MSME 100% owned by SC or ST entrepreneurs, with Udyam registration and a term loan from a bank or RBI-regulated financial institution.

What you can get
A capital subsidy of 10%–25% of the term loan (up to ₹35 lakh) and an interest subsidy of 6%–8% a year for 5 years (up to ₹30 lakh a year).
Rates are higher outside Municipal Corporation areas, and founders under 35 get an extra 1% interest subsidy.

Capital subsidy is for manufacturing only; eligible service enterprises get a 6% interest subsidy (max ₹25 lakh a year for 5 years). Entrepreneurs under 35 on the loan-sanction date get an extra 1% interest subsidy. Rates are from the 2017 resolution; the scheme was continued by a 2020 resolution — confirm the current version with your DIC. Under the Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026, SC/ST entrepreneurs are also reported to get an extra 5% on the incentive ceiling.

Who it’s for

Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs setting up or expanding manufacturing or eligible service MSMEs anywhere in Gujarat.

Eligibility

  • Enterprise 100% owned by SC/ST entrepreneur(s) aged over 18
  • MSME under the MSMED Act with Udyam registration
  • Term loan from a bank or RBI-regulated financial institution — NBFC loans not accepted
  • At least 85% local workers and 50% local supervisory/managerial staff
  • New assets only — loans on second-hand machinery are not eligible
  • One expansion/diversification and one modernisation allowed

How to apply

  1. Get the term loan sanctioned and disbursed for the project
  2. Apply at your District Industries Centre within the time limit in the GR
  3. Submit commercial-production proof for capital subsidy
  4. Claim interest subsidy periodically with the bank’s interest certificate
  5. Keep the unit in production for at least 5 years

Frequently asked

Who is eligible for Ambedkar Udhyog Uday Yojana?
MSMEs 100% owned by Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs aged over 18, with Udyam registration, financed by a bank or RBI-regulated financial institution (not an NBFC).
How much capital subsidy can I get?
20% or 25% of the term loan for plant and machinery up to ₹50 lakh (max ₹25 lakh inside Municipal Corporation areas, ₹35 lakh outside), and 10% or 15% for larger investments within the same caps.
What is the interest subsidy?
6% a year (max ₹25 lakh a year) inside Municipal Corporation areas and 8% (max ₹30 lakh a year) outside, for five years, plus 1% more if you are under 35.
Can I combine it with Stand-Up India?
Yes — Stand-Up India is a central bank-loan scheme and this is a state subsidy on such loans. The total of state and central subsidy cannot exceed the loan or the interest actually paid.
