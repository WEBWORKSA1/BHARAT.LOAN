CMEGP: Chief Minister’s Employment Generation Programme (Maharashtra)

Chief Minister’s Employment Generation Programme · Directorate of Industries · DIC & KVIB, Maharashtra

Maharashtra’s flagship self-employment scheme — a bank loan for a brand-new unit with a 15–35% subsidy on project cost, for manufacturing projects up to ₹50 lakh and service projects up to ₹10 lakh.

Quick eligibility read

Maharashtra resident aged 18–45 (50 for special categories) starting a new manufacturing, service or agro-based unit, who has never availed PMEGP or another subsidy-linked scheme. One person per family.

What you can get
A margin-money subsidy of 15–35% of project cost, credited against a bank term loan for a new unit — up to ₹50 lakh project cost for manufacturing, ₹10 lakh for service, agro-based and other eligible ventures. Banks fund 90% (general) or 95% (special category) of project cost.
Implemented by the Directorate of Industries through District Industries Centres (urban areas and all projects above ₹25 lakh) and the Maharashtra State Khadi & Village Industries Board (rural areas). Ready-built shed or workshop cost is capped at 20% of project cost; for service/agro projects working capital can be up to 30%. Projects without capital expenditure are not eligible.

Age limit relaxed by 5 years for special categories. Annual targets reserve 20% for SC/ST, 30% for women and 3% for persons with disabilities.

Who it’s for

First-time entrepreneurs in Maharashtra — individuals (proprietorships), partnerships and registered self-help groups — setting up a new manufacturing, service, agro-processing or similar unit with institutional finance.

Eligibility

  • Resident of Maharashtra (domicile certificate if born outside the state)
  • Aged 18–45 years; relaxed by 5 years for special categories
  • New project only — existing units are not eligible
  • Not availed PMEGP, PMRY, REGP or any other subsidy-linked central/state scheme
  • Education: 7th pass for projects ₹10–25 lakh; 10th pass above ₹25 lakh
  • One applicant per family (self and spouse); no income ceiling

How to apply

  1. Prepare a project report (manufacturing ≤ ₹50 lakh / service ≤ ₹10 lakh; land excluded)
  2. Register and apply online at maha-cmegp.gov.in — physical forms are not accepted
  3. DIC (urban / >₹25 lakh) or KVIB (rural) scrutinises and forwards the file to your chosen bank
  4. Bank appraises and sanctions the loan; complete any required EDP/skill training
  5. Subsidy is released by the nodal agency against the bank loan as per guidelines

Frequently asked

How much subsidy does CMEGP give?
15% (urban) or 25% (rural) of project cost for general-category applicants, and 25% (urban) or 35% (rural) for special categories — SC/ST, women, ex-servicemen and persons with disabilities. You contribute 10% (general) or 5% (special); the bank lends the rest.
What is the maximum project cost?
Up to ₹50 lakh for manufacturing, and up to ₹10 lakh for service, agro-based and other eligible ventures. Land is not part of project cost.
Can I get CMEGP if I already took PMEGP?
No. Existing units, and anyone who has already availed PMEGP, PMRY, REGP or another subsidy-linked central or state scheme, are not eligible.
Is there an education requirement?
For projects above ₹10 lakh up to ₹25 lakh you need at least a 7th-standard pass; above ₹25 lakh, at least 10th-standard pass.
Where do I apply?
Only online, on the official CMEGP portal (maha-cmegp.gov.in). Physical applications are not accepted. DIC handles urban areas and all projects above ₹25 lakh; the Khadi & Village Industries Board handles rural applications.
Where an advisor helps

CMEGP is generous but strict: one shot per family, no second subsidy scheme, and banks judge the project report hard. The urban/rural and general/special split changes the subsidy by up to 20 percentage points, and project-cost rules (shed cap, working-capital cap, land excluded) trip many files. We structure the project report to bank standards, pick the right implementing agency and track the file to sanction.
