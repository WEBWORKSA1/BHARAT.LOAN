Questions

Is my contribution tax-deductible?
No. BHARAT.LOAN is an independent publisher, not a registered charity, so contributions are voluntary support payments and are not eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G.
What exactly does my support pay for?
Research and fact-checking of scheme rules, keeping pages updated when limits change, Hindi and regional-language content, free tools like the Funding Matcher, outreach and marketing to reach more small businesses, hiring writers and video creators, and prizes for community contests.
Do supporters get anything in return?
Supporters are thanked on our supporters wall (only if you want to be listed) and get early access to new tools and guides. Supporting the site never affects which schemes we recommend.
Can my company sponsor instead?
Yes — sponsorships, contest prizes and branded content are handled separately. Use the sponsorship contact link at the top of any page.

Get new schemes & deadlines first

One short email when something changes — new schemes, revised limits, application windows. No spam.
