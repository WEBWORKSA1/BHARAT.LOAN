Gujarat schemes & incentives

Gujarat runs some of the country's most generous industrial and MSME incentive policies — capital subsidies, interest subvention, and SGST-linked benefits that scale with taluka category. Our advisory desk works Gujarat on the ground, sector by sector.

Gujarat schemes we cover

Subsidy · Tier S
Aatmanirbhar Gujarat (MSME)
Gujarat's flagship MSME package — interest subsidy up to 7%, plus capital subsidy and net SGST reimbursement, all scaled by taluka category.
Subsidy · Tier S
Gujarat Textile Policy 2024
Capital subsidy of 10%–35% of investment plus a 5%–7% interest subsidy across the textile value chain — one of India's most generous textile packages.
Subsidy · Tier A
Gujarat Large Industry Incentive
For projects investing above ₹50 crore — net SGST reimbursement up to 100% for 10 years, plus a 7% interest subsidy, scaled by taluka and sector.
Tax · Tier A
Gujarat Startup Scheme
₹20,000/month sustenance plus seed support up to ₹30 lakh, mentorship and accelerator funding for Gujarat-based startups.
