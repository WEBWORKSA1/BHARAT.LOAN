New from 1 June 2026

The Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 replaces the 2022 Aatmanirbhar schemes for new units. MSMEs choose a mix of capital subsidy (25%–35%), 7% interest subsidy and power-tariff support up to 45% of eligible investment; large units get 15%–35% and selected thrust sectors up to 50%. Expired, closed & awaited

So you don’t waste time on a scheme that is no longer open.

  • Gujarat Tourism Policy 2021-25 — lapsed on 31 December 2025. A new tourism policy is being prepared ahead of the 2030 Commonwealth Games; until it is notified, hotel and resort projects should check the 2026 industrial policy and central schemes.
  • Diamond-unit relief package (May 2025) — 9% interest subsidy for three years on term loans up to ₹5 lakh, one-year electricity-duty exemption and school-fee support for unemployed artisans; the two-month application window has closed.
  • Aatmanirbhar Gujarat schemes 2022 (MSME and large industries) — closed to units starting production from 1 June 2026; existing beneficiaries and opted-in projects continue (production cut-off 4 October 2027).
  • Agribusiness & Export Promotion Policy 2026 — announced in the 2026-27 budget with a token ₹10 crore provision; detailed incentives awaited.

Frequently asked

How many government funding schemes are there for businesses in Gujarat?
Gujarat has well over 20 business-funding routes: the Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026 for manufacturing units of every size; sector policies for textiles, IT/ITeS, GCCs, electronics, semiconductors, biotech and space; self-employment schemes such as Shri Vajpayee Bankable Yojana and Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana; loans from community development corporations; student-startup grants under SSIP 2.0; and 148 iKhedut components for farmers and fishers — on top of central schemes like PMEGP and Mudra.
What replaced the Aatmanirbhar Gujarat scheme?
The Viksit Gujarat Industrial Policy 2026, effective 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2031. It replaces net-SGST reimbursement with a choice of capital subsidy, 7% interest subsidy and power-tariff support worth up to 45% of investment for MSMEs. Units already under the 2022 scheme continue on it.
Which Gujarat scheme is best for a small business with no collateral?
For a new small unit, Shri Vajpayee Bankable Yojana gives a bank loan up to ₹25 lakh with a 20%–40% subsidy and no income limit. Pair it with CGTMSE cover for collateral-free credit, or compare with PMEGP.
Are there Gujarat schemes only for women?
Yes — Mukhyamantri Mahila Utkarsh Yojana (interest-free ₹1 lakh loans for women’s groups), Mahila Swavlamban Yojana (₹2 lakh loan with 15% subsidy), higher SVBY subsidy (30%–40%) and, under the 2026 industrial policy, an extra 1% interest subsidy and rent assistance up to ₹3 lakh a year for women entrepreneurs.
Where do I apply for Gujarat schemes?
Most business subsidies go through your District Industries Centre (DIC) or the Industries Commissionerate; sector policies through the Department of Science & Technology or the Gujarat State Electronics/Biotechnology Missions; corporation loans through the e-Samaj Kalyan, GUEEDC, GBCDC or GMFDC portals; and farm subsidies through iKhedut.
Which Gujarat schemes have expired?
The Gujarat Tourism Policy 2021-25 lapsed on 31 December 2025 and a new policy is awaited; the 2025 relief package for small diamond units had a short application window that has closed; and the 2022 Aatmanirbhar schemes are closed to units starting production from 1 June 2026.
