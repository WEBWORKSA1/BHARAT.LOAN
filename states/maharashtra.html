Maharashtra schemes & incentives

India’s industrial heartland runs three very different funding routes: the CMEGP subsidy for first-time entrepreneurs, interest reimbursement on business loans through the Annasaheb Patil corporation, and the new Industries, Investment & Services Policy 2025 with location-based incentives worth up to 100% of investment.

Location decides the money

Under the IIS Policy 2025 the overall incentive ceiling ranges from 30% of fixed capital investment in Group A talukas to 100% in aspirational districts. Vidarbha, Marathwada, Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg, Jalgaon and Dhule get 80%. See the zone table →
