Uttar Pradesh schemes & incentives

Uttar Pradesh runs India's deepest MSME and self-employment incentive stack — from the interest-free CM-YUVA youth loan and the district-segmented ODOP programme to the UPIEPP 2022 capital-subsidy / SGST policy for large industry. UP is the #1 state by MSME unit count and by PMEGP disbursement. Our advisory desk works UP district by district, matching you to the right scheme and nodal agency.

Uttar Pradesh schemes we cover

Credit · Tier S
CM-YUVA (Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Vikas Abhiyan)
UP's flagship youth scheme — a 0% interest, collateral-free loan up to ₹5 lakh, with a 10% subsidy on timely repayment and the state covering your CGTMSE cost.
Subsidy · Tier S
ODOP (One District One Product)
UP's signature programme — margin-money support up to ₹6.25 lakh for units making their district's designated product, across all 75 districts.
Subsidy · Tier A
Mukhyamantri Yuva Swarozgar Yojana (CMYSY)
Margin-money subsidy for new UP enterprises — up to ₹6.25 lakh (industry) / ₹2.50 lakh (service), on projects up to ₹25 lakh.
Subsidy · Tier A
UP Industrial Investment & Employment Promotion Policy 2022
UP's umbrella large-industry policy — a one-time choice of capital subsidy, net SGST reimbursement, or PLI top-up, for Large / Mega / Super-Mega / Ultra-Mega projects.
Subsidy · Tier B
UP MSME Promotion Policy 2022
UP's MSME support stack — infrastructure interest subsidy, quality-certification and patent reimbursement, ERP/ICT support, and regional stamp-duty exemptions.
Subsidy · Tier B
UP Textile & Garmenting Policy 2022
Sector incentives for UP's textile belt — Varanasi, Bhadohi, Kanpur, Lucknow — targeting ₹10,000 crore of new investment in garments and textiles.
